### 1. 利用自监督表征的语音作为辅助诊断重度抑郁症的生物标志物

- **英文标题**: Speech as a biomarker for supported diagnosis of major depressive disorder using self-supervised representations.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Yunhan Lin, Biman Najika Liyanage, Chuan Shi
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42297825/

**中文摘要**: 重度抑郁症（MDD）的诊断迫切需要可用于临床转化的客观生物标志物。本研究建立了迄今为止规模最大的多中心队列（样本量N=1,816，包含910例MDD患者和906例健康对照者），利用23,608份标准化语音样本。基于6,373个声学韵律特征，我们开发了一种采用自监督架构的深度学习框架，以利用语音生物标志物。我们对预训练基础模型（包括WavLM和HuBERT）与传统声学特征（通过openSMILE提取）进行了系统比较分析。模型性能通过受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC）、敏感性和特异性进行评估。我们的框架在内部验证（n=333）中实现了0.932的AUC，显著优于传统方法，同时在外部验证（n=160，AUC=0.879）中保持了有效性。与其他模型相比，自监督表征展现出稳健的诊断准确性。利用大规模语音生物标志物数据集，我们的研究结果为辅助抑郁评估提供了一种快速、经济且非侵入性的方法。

**英文摘要**: The diagnosis of major depressive disorder (MDD) urgently requires objective biomarkers for clinical translation. In this study, we established a multicenter cohort to date (N = 1,816; comprising 910 MDD patients and 906 healthy control subjects), using 23,608 standardized speech samples. Based on 6,373 acoustic-prosodic features, we develop a deep learning framework that employs a self-supervised architecture to leverage speech biomarkers. We perform a systematic comparative analysis among pretrained foundation models, including WavLM and HuBERT, and traditional acoustic features extracted from openSMILE. Model performance is evaluated using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC), sensitivity, and specificity. Our framework achieves an AUC of 0.932 in internal validation (n = 333), significantly outperforming conventional methods, while maintaining efficacy in external validation (n = 160, AUC = 0.879). Self-supervised representations demonstrate robust diagnostic accuracy compared to other models. Leveraging the large speech biomarker dataset, our findings provide a rapid, cost-effective, and non-invasive approach for assisted depression assessment.

### 2. 面向放射科医生的零样本胸部肿瘤病史生成：基于检索增强的大语言模型流水线。

- **英文标题**: Zero-shot Thoracic Oncologic History Generation for Radiologists Using Retrieval-augmented Large Language Model Pipeline.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Karan Jani, Govind Mattay, Vamsi Narra
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42301010/

**中文摘要**: 背景：收集肿瘤病史是必要的但效率低下。近期利用大型语言模型（LLM）生成临床摘要的努力依赖于手动信息检索、提示调优和主观评估，限制了临床转化。  
目的：开发并评估一种零样本LLM流水线，该流水线通过检索增强生成技术程序化地生成结构化的肿瘤病史，以约束LLM的输出。  
材料与方法：回顾性收集2018年1月至2024年4月期间某癌症中心胸部肿瘤患者的电子健康记录数据。20名接受调查的放射科医生选择了10项待总结的临床参数。开发了一种检索增强生成流水线以筛选临床数据。三个符合《健康保险便携性与责任法案》的LLM——OpenAI的GPT-4o mini、o3-mini和GPT-5-mini——使用定义的数据模式和零样本提示方法生成结构化摘要。以检索到的临床文本为标准对摘要进行人工评估，完整性的参考标准基于GPT-5-mini的输出。两名放射科住院医师被计时总结临床参数，建立了240秒的基准时间。模型间比较采用配对t检验评估准确性、完整性、成本和处理时间。利用人工提取时间、自动摘要生成时间以及全国放射科医生生产力调查数据进行成本效益分析。  
结果：在2433例患者中，随机选取50例进行LLM摘要生成（平均年龄67.0岁 ± 12.5 [标准差]；32例女性）。GPT-5-mini流水线的平均完整度得分最高，为95.5%（95% CI: 94.2, 96.7），而o3-mini的平均准确率相似，为97.9%（95% CI: 96.8, 99.1）。GPT-4o mini的每份摘要处理时间最快，平均12.7秒，而基准时间为240秒（P < .001）。理想情况下，这些时间节省预计可为每位放射科医生带来每年19,525至70,942美元的增量收入，成本为30.26至32.46美元。  
结论：该LLM流水线无需手动微调或信息检索，即可准确总结肿瘤病史。这些摘要的完整性和速度取决于所使用的LLM。

**英文摘要**: Background Gathering oncologic history is necessary but inefficient. Recent efforts to generate clinical summaries using large language models (LLMs) have relied on manual information retrieval, prompt tuning, and subjective evaluation, limiting clinical translation. Purpose To develop and evaluate a zero-shot LLM pipeline that programmatically generates structured oncologic histories using retrieval-augmented generation to ground LLM outputs. Materials and Methods Retrospective electronic health record data were collected for thoracic oncology patients at a cancer center from January 2018 to April 2024. Twenty surveyed radiologists selected 10 clinical parameters for summarization. A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline was developed to filter clinical data. Three Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act-compliant LLMs-OpenAI's GPT-4o mini, o3-mini, and GPT-5-mini-produced structured summaries using a defined data schema and a zero-shot prompt approach. Summaries were manually evaluated against retrieved clinical text, with completeness reference standard based on the output of GPT-5-mini. Two radiology residents were timed summarizing clinical parameters, establishing a 240-second benchmark. Comparisons between models were conducted using paired t tests for accuracy, completeness, cost, and processing time. Cost-benefit analyses were conducted using manual extraction time, automated summary generation time, and national survey data on radiologist productivity. Results Of 2433 patients, 50 were randomly selected for LLM summarization (mean age, 67.0 years ± 12.5 [SD]; 32 female patients). The GPT-5-mini pipeline achieved the highest mean completeness score of 95.5% (95% CI: 94.2, 96.7) and was similar to o3-mini for a mean accuracy of 97.9% (95% CI: 96.8, 99.1). GPT-4o mini had the fastest processing time per summary, averaging 12.7 seconds compared with the 240-second benchmark ( P < .001). Ideally, these time savings project to annual incremental revenue of $19 525 to $70 942 per radiologist, with costs of $30.26 to $32.46. Conclusion The LLM pipeline, which does not require manual fine-tuning or information retrieval, accurately summarizes oncologic history. The completeness and speed of these summaries vary depending on the LLM used. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article. ### 3. 深度学习检测增强和非增强CT上与胰腺癌相关的直接和间接影像学表现

- **英文标题**: Deep Learning Detection of Direct and Indirect Imaging Findings Associated with Pancreatic Cancer at Contrast-enhanced and Noncontrast CT.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Takeru Yamaguchi, Keitaro Sofue, Atsuhiro Masuda
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42301012/

**中文摘要**: **背景** 深度学习（DL）模型在胰腺癌（PC）诊断中展现出前景；然而，目前尚缺乏能够同时检测与PC相关的直接和间接影像学征象的模型。  
**目的** 开发并评估可检测非增强CT（NCCT）和增强CT（CECT）图像上直接与间接影像学征象的DL模型，用于胰腺癌诊断。  
**材料与方法** 本回顾性研究纳入2007年8月至2022年12月期间的PC患者及对照患者。利用NCCT和CECT图像开发了两个DL模型，用于检测直接征象（胰腺肿块）和间接征象（实质萎缩、主胰管[MPD]扩张及MPD狭窄），并基于这些征象诊断PC。训练和验证阶段对来自多家机构的CT扫描图像进行人工标注。模型评估采用两个外部测试集（CECT和NCCT测试集）。采用受试者工作特征曲线分析评价诊断性能。将模型在检测影像学征象和PC方面的表现与六名医师的表现进行比较。PC诊断的金标准为组织病理学证实。  
**结果** 本研究共纳入2251例患者（平均年龄66岁±13.3[SD]；年龄范围20-96岁；男性850例）。DL模型在CECT测试集中检测胰腺肿块、实质萎缩、MPD扩张和MPD狭窄的受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC）值分别为0.94、0.90、0.94和0.94；在NCCT测试集中分别为0.88、0.88、0.95和0.93。在PC诊断方面，DL模型的表现在CECT测试集（AUC：0.99 vs. 0.99；P=0.84）和NCCT测试集（AUC：0.93 vs. 0.91；P=0.03）中与六名读者的平均表现相当或更优。对于直径≤20mm的PC，DL模型在CECT测试集（98% vs. 82.6%；P<0.001）和NCCT测试集（86% vs. 41.1%；P<0.001）中均显示出比读者均值更高的敏感性。  
**结论** DL模型可检测CT图像上的直接和间接影像学征象，并以与医师相当或更优的性能诊断PC，尤其在诊断小胰腺癌方面表现突出。© RSNA, 2026

**英文摘要**: Background Deep learning (DL) models have shown promise in diagnosing pancreatic cancer (PC); however, models that simultaneously detect both direct and indirect imaging findings associated with PC are lacking. Purpose To develop and evaluate DL models that detect direct and indirect imaging findings on noncontrast CT (NCCT) and contrast-enhanced CT (CECT) images for PC diagnosis. Materials and Methods This retrospective study from August 2007 to December 2022 included patients with PC and control patients. Two DL models were developed using NCCT and CECT to detect direct (pancreatic mass) and indirect (parenchymal atrophy, main pancreatic duct [MPD] dilatation, and MPD stenosis) imaging findings and diagnose PC based on these findings. For training and validation, CT scans from multiple institutions were manually annotated. Model evaluation was performed using two external test sets (CECT and NCCT sets). Receiver operating characteristic curve analysis was used to assess diagnostic performance. Model performance in detecting imaging findings and PC was compared with the performance of six physicians. The reference standard for PC diagnosis was histopathologic confirmation. Results This study included 2251 patients (mean age, 66 years ± 13.3 [SD]; age range, 20-96 years; 850 men). DL models demonstrated area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) values of 0.94, 0.90, 0.94, and 0.94 in the CECT set and 0.88, 0.88, 0.95, and 0.93 in the NCCT set for detecting pancreatic masses, parenchymal atrophy, MPD dilatation, and MPD stenosis, respectively. For PC diagnosis, DL models performed similarly to or better than the mean of six readers in the CECT (AUC, 0.99 vs 0.99; P = .84) and NCCT (AUC, 0.93 vs 0.91; P = .03) sets. For PCs that were 20 mm or smaller, the DL models demonstrated higher sensitivity than the reader mean in both the CECT (98% vs 82.6%; P < .001) and NCCT (86% vs 41.1%; P < .001) sets. Conclusion DL models detected direct and indirect imaging findings on CT images and diagnosed PC with performance comparable to or better than that of physicians, particularly for small PCs. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article. See also the editorial by Bhayana and Rajpurkar in this issue.

### 4. 放射学重塑：从影像AI实践演示中汲取的互操作性与经验教训

- **作者**: Albert Jiao, Mohannad Hussain, Teri Sippel Schmidt

北美放射学会（RSNA）年会上展示了“放射学再想象”（Radiology Reimagined）示范项目（原名“实践中的影像AI”）。该项目强调互操作性与语义标准如何促进人工智能（AI）融入放射学工作流程。这种整合涵盖检查申请、排程、方案制定、图像采集、检查解读、报告生成、结果沟通、随访及计费等环节。自2020年起，“放射学再想象”已成为RSNA年会的显著特色，展示了AI在临床影像工作流程中的作用。该项目推动了放射学与行业供应商合作伙伴之间的协作，促进了诸如医学数字成像与通信（DICOM）、健康等级7快速医疗互操作性资源（FHIR）以及医疗企业集成（IHE）规范等标准的采纳。通过逼真的临床场景，该项目展示了将AI工具整合至医疗点系统（point-of-care systems）的实用价值与挑战。

“放射学再想象”场景的复杂性逐年提升：从2020年简单的肺结节和颅内出血检测，发展到2024年前列腺MRI解读及淀粉样蛋白相关影像异常检测。每年均有超过20家独特的供应商合作伙伴参与，逐步纳入新标准，例如2023年引入的集成多媒体报告（Integrated Multimedia Reporting），以及2024年对FHIRcast（用于实时同步医疗保健应用程序）的改进支持。参与者学习如何促进互操作性并将软件整合至其临床工作流程中，重点聚焦于实用建议与关键考量。

该示范项目的未来迭代计划包括扩大参会者参与度、系统收集定量反馈，以及拓宽新兴标准的应用范围。每年示范活动的视频可在RSNA官方网站上获取。

**英文摘要**: The Radiology Reimagined demonstration, formerly known as Imaging AI in Practice, is presented at the Radiological Society of North America (RSNA) Annual Meeting. It highlights how interoperability and semantic standards can facilitate the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into radiology workflows. This integration encompasses examination ordering, scheduling, protocoling, image acquisition, examination interpretation, reporting, results communication, follow-up, and billing. Since 2020, Radiology Reimagined has become a prominent feature of the RSNA Annual Meeting, showcasing AI's role in clinical imaging workflows. The demonstration fosters collaboration between radiology and industry vendor partners, promoting the adoption of standards such as Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine, Health Level 7 Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR), and Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise profiles. Realistic clinical scenarios demonstrate the utility and challenges of integrating AI tools into point-of-care systems. The complexity of Radiology Reimagined scenarios has increased, from simple pulmonary nodule and intracranial hemorrhage detection in 2020 to prostate MRI interpretation and amyloid-related imaging abnormality detection in 2024. Each year, more than 20 unique vendor partners participate, progressively incorporating new standards such as Integrated Multimedia Reporting in 2023 and improved support for FHIRcast (which synchronizes health care applications in real time) in 2024. Participants learn how to facilitate interoperability and integrate software into their clinical workflows, with an emphasis on practical advice and key considerations. Future iterations of the demonstration will aim to expand attendee engagement, formalize quantitative feedback, and broaden the use of emerging standards. Videos of the annual demonstrations are available on the RSNA website.
