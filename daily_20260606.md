### 1. 一种用于有依据的生物医学图像解读的通用基础模型。

- **英文标题**: A universal foundation model for grounded biomedical image interpretation.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Linshan Wu, Yuxiang Nie, Sunan He
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 9/10 — 医学影像AI基础模型用于生成可解释的诊断发现并定位目标，AI核心贡献且临床价值高
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42243102/

**中文摘要**: 将人工智能辅助的生物医学图像分析整合到临床实践中，要求人工智能生成的结果不仅准确，还需具备可解释性。然而，现有模型通常无法同时生成诊断发现并定位对应目标，这一局限使得将AI生成结果与视觉证据关联并进行结果解读变得困难。为此，我们提出UniBiomed——一种通用的基础模型，专为基于证据的生物医学图像解读而设计，能够生成精确的诊断发现并分割生物医学目标。UniBiomed基于多模态大语言模型与分割一切模型的集成，通过统一训练可整合多种生物医学任务，从而推动基于证据的图像解读发展。为开发UniBiomed，我们构建了包含2700万组图像、区域标注与文本描述三元组的大规模数据集。在70个内部数据集和14个外部数据集上的广泛验证表明，UniBiomed在多项生物医学任务中均达到最先进性能。

**英文摘要**: The integration of AI-assisted biomedical image analysis into clinical practice demands AI-generated findings that are not only accurate but also interpretable. However, existing models generally lack the ability to simultaneously generate diagnostic findings and localize corresponding targets. This limitation makes it challenging to correlate AI-generated findings with visual evidence and interpret the results. To this end, we introduce UniBiomed, a universal foundation model for grounded biomedical image interpretation, which is capable of generating accurate diagnostic findings and segmenting the biomedical targets. UniBiomed is based on an integration of Multi-modal Large Language Model and Segment Anything Model, which can unify diverse biomedical tasks in universal training for advancing grounded interpretation. To develop UniBiomed, we curate a large-scale dataset comprising 27 million triplets of images, region annotations, and text descriptions. Extensive validation on 70 internal and 14 external datasets demonstrated the state-of-the-art performance of UniBiomed in diverse biomedical tasks.

### 2. 多角度闪烁成像下放射照片的AI辅助分割与体积重建

- **英文标题**: AI-Assisted segmentation and volumetric reconstruction of radiographs through multi-angular scintillation imaging.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Seokho Kim, Byungjoon Bae, Do Wan Kim
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 7/10 — AI辅助医学影像分割与体积重建，虽未明确临床场景但属于医学影像AI技术方向
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42243100/

**中文摘要**: X射线成像是一种用于非破坏性检测的基本工具。尽管常规X射线摄影非常适合二维成像，但它无法提供体积结构信息。计算机断层扫描虽能实现三维重建，但仍受限于庞大的设备、高辐射暴露和成本问题。在此，我们展示了一种与多级神经网络集成的贴片型闪烁体，该网络可从稀疏角度的二维X射线照片中分割并重建三维体积。该闪烁体通过将纤维素纳米晶体电喷雾到块状纤维素基质上，随后浸涂钙钛矿制成，形成一种在X射线激发下具有增强辐射发光性能的复合材料。这种柔性薄膜能够贴合复杂几何形状，实现无畸变的多角度成像。神经网络在合成数据集上训练，并在实验获取的鸟类跗跖骨X射线照片上验证，能够准确重建体积骨骼结构。该方法为低剂量、可获取的基于人工智能的三维X射线成像提供了概念验证，证明了仅从三个稀疏投影中恢复宏观三维形态的可行性。

**英文摘要**: X-ray imaging serves as a fundamental tool for non-destructive inspection. Although conventional radiography is well suited for two-dimensional imaging, it cannot provide volumetric structure. Computed tomography provides three-dimensional reconstruction but remains constrained by bulky instrumentation, high radiation exposure, and cost. Here we demonstrate a patch-type scintillator integrated with multi-stage neural network that segments and reconstructs three-dimensional volumes from sparse angular two-dimensional radiographs. The scintillator is fabricated by electrospraying cellulose nanocrystals onto a bulk cellulose matrix, followed by dip-coating of perovskite, yielding a composite with enhanced radioluminescence under X-ray excitation. This flexible film conforms to complex geometries, enabling distortion-free and multi-angle imaging. Neural networks are trained on synthetic datasets and validated on experimentally acquired avian tibiotarsus radiographs, accurately reconstructing volumetric bone structures. This approach serves as a proof-of-concept for low-dose, accessible artificial intelligence-enabled three-dimensional X-ray imaging, demonstrating the feasibility of recovering macroscopic three-dimensional morphology from as few as three sparse projections.
