### 1. 一种面向临床的术中病理基础模型

- **英文标题**: A clinically-oriented foundation model for intraoperative pathology.
- **期刊**: Nature medicine (2026)
- **作者**: Zihan Zhao, Fengtao Zhou, Ronggang Li
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 9/10 — 病理影像基础模型用于术中冰冻切片诊断，属医学影像AI临床诊断核心方向
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42742185/

**中文摘要**: 术中病理对精准外科至关重要，然而其临床影响受到诊断复杂性及高质量冰冻切片数据有限可及性的制约。尽管计算病理学已取得重大进展，但缺乏大规模前瞻性验证阻碍了其在外科工作流程中的常规应用。在此，我们介绍CRISP——一种基于来自十家医疗中心的超过100,000张冰冻切片开发的面向临床的基础模型，专门设计用于为病理学提供面向临床的稳健术中支持（Clinically-oriented Robust Intraoperative Support for Pathology, CRISP）。CRISP在超过15,000张术中切片上，针对近100项回顾性诊断任务进行了全面评估，包括良恶性鉴别、关键术中决策和泛癌检测等。该模型在6家机构、14种肿瘤类型和24个解剖部位（包括此前未见过的部位和罕见癌症）中展现出稳健的泛化能力。在一个纳入超过3,000例患者的前瞻性队列中，CRISP在真实世界条件下保持了高诊断准确性，并在92.6%的病例中直接为手术决策提供信息。人机协作进一步将诊断工作量减少了35%，避免了105项辅助检测，并以87.5%的准确率增强了微转移灶的检测。总之，这些发现表明，CRISP代表了一种面向临床的AI驱动术中病理学方法，具有支持手术决策并促进计算方法向临床实践转化的潜力。

**英文摘要**: Intraoperative pathology is pivotal to precision surgery, yet its clinical impact is constrained by diagnostic complexity and the limited availability of high-quality frozen-section data. While computational pathology has made significant strides, the lack of large-scale, prospective validation has impeded its routine adoption in surgical workflows. Here, we introduce CRISP, a clinically oriented foundation model developed on over 100,000 frozen sections from ten medical centers, specifically designed to provide Clinically-oriented Robust Intraoperative Support for Pathology (CRISP). CRISP was comprehensively evaluated on more than 15,000 intraoperative slides across nearly 100 retrospective diagnostic tasks, including benign-malignant discrimination, key intraoperative decision-making, and pan-cancer detection, etc. The model demonstrated robust generalization across 6 institutions, 14 tumor types, and 24 anatomical sites-including previously unseen sites and rare cancers. In a prospective cohort of over 3,000 patients, CRISP sustained high diagnostic accuracy under real-world conditions, directly informing surgical decisions in 92.6% of cases. Human-AI collaboration further reduced diagnostic workload by 35%, avoided 105 ancillary tests and enhanced detection of micrometastases with 87.5% accuracy. Together, these findings suggest that CRISP represents a clinically oriented approach to AI-driven intraoperative pathology, with the potential to support surgical decision-making and facilitate the translation of computational methods into clinical practice.

### 2. 以GPT-5-Thinking弥合全科医师与亚专科医师之间的差距：眼眶及头颈部肿瘤MRI报告的双中心评估

- **英文标题**: Bridging the Generalist-Subspecialist Gap with GPT-5-Thinking: Dual-Center Evaluation in Orbital and Head-and-Neck Tumor MRI Reports.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Jie Li, Liang Xiao, Xiaoxia Qu
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42742382/

**中文摘要**: 背景　在复杂区域识别肿瘤组织学亚型对全科放射科医师而言具有挑战性。推理型大语言模型能否通过解读影像学描述来弥合这一专业差距，目前尚缺乏深入探索。目的　评估生成式预训练变换器（GPT）-5-Thinking能否表现出与专科医师相似的水平，并帮助弥合全科医师在眼眶及头颈部肿瘤MRI扫描解读中的专业差距。材料与方法　这项双中心回顾性研究在一家全科实践中心和一家专科实践中心进行，纳入了便利抽样中经病理证实为眼眶或头颈部肿瘤且具有治疗前MRI报告的患者。GPT-5-Thinking处理叙述性MRI报告，输出良恶性分类及前3位鉴别诊断。第1阶段比较了模型在在全科和专科实践环境中与常规临床报告诊断的准确性。第2阶段中，七名全科医师对选定的肿瘤报告进行了两次解读，分别在无和有GPT-5-Thinking辅助的情况下进行。采用McNemar检验和混合效应logistic回归模型进行分析。结果　本研究纳入1000例患者（平均年龄±SD，51岁±17.0；男性520例）。在第1阶段，GPT-5-Thinking在眼眶肿瘤患者中的top-1准确率与专科医师相似（76.7%[300例中230例] vs 78.3%[300例中235例]；P = .64），在头颈部肿瘤患者中亦然（67.5%[200例中135例] vs 68.0%[200例中136例]；P = .92）。对于眼眶肿瘤患者，GPT-5-Thinking的top-1准确率高于常规全科医师（74.0%[300例中222例] vs 68.7%[300例中206例]；P = .03）；对于头颈部肿瘤患者亦然（64.0%[200例中128例] vs 27.0%[200例中54例]；P < .001）。在第2阶段，GPT-5-Thinking辅助与全科医师top-1诊断准确率的提高相关：眼眶肿瘤患者从61.4%（700例中430例）提高至70.3%（700例中492例）（P < .001），头颈部肿瘤患者从47.0%（700例中329例）提高至61.0%（700例中427例）（P < .001）。结论　在使用眼眶或头颈部肿瘤患者的MRI报告时，生成式预训练变换器（GPT）-5-Thinking表现出与专科医师相似的诊断准确率；GPT-5-Thinking辅助也与全科医师诊断准确率的提高相关。© RSNA, 2026　本文附有补充材料。

**英文摘要**: Background Identifying tumor histologic subtypes in complex regions is challenging for generalist radiologists. Whether reasoning large language models can bridge this expertise gap by interpreting radiologic descriptions remains underexplored. Purpose To evaluate whether Generative Pretrained Transformer (GPT)-5-Thinking can perform similarly to subspecialists and help bridge the generalist expertise gap in interpretation of MRI scans of orbital and head-and-neck tumors. Materials and Methods This dual-center retrospective study was performed at a generalist practice center and a subspecialist practice center and included convenience series of patients with pathologically confirmed orbital or head-and-neck tumors who had pretreatment MRI reports. GPT-5-Thinking processed narrative MRI reports to output benign-malignant classification and top-3 differential diagnoses. Phase 1 compared model accuracy with routine clinical report diagnoses in generalist and subspecialist practice settings. In Phase 2, seven generalists interpreted selected tumor reports twice, without and with GPT-5-Thinking assistance. McNemar tests and mixed-effects logistic regression models were used for analysis. Results This study included 1000 patients (mean age ± SD, 51 years ± 17.0; 520 men). In phase 1, the top-1 accuracy of GPT-5-Thinking was similar to that of subspecialists in patients with orbital tumors (76.7% [230 of 300] vs 78.3% [235 of 300]; P = .64) and head-and-neck tumors (67.5% [135 of 200] vs 68.0% [136 of 200]; P = .92). GPT-5-Thinking had higher top-1 accuracy than did routine generalists for patients with orbital tumors (74.0% [222 of 300] vs 68.7% [206 of 300]; P = .03) and head-and-neck tumors (64.0% [128 of 200] vs 27.0% [54 of 200]; P < .001). In phase 2, GPT-5-Thinking assistance was associated with an increase in generalist top-1 diagnostic accuracy from 61.4% (430 of 700) to 70.3% (492 of 700) ( P < .001) in patients with orbital tumors and from 47.0% (329 of 700) to 61.0% (427 of 700) ( P < .001) in patients with head-and-neck tumors. Conclusion Using MRI reports of patients with orbital or head-and-neck tumors, Generative Pretrained Transformer (GPT)-5-Thinking showed diagnostic accuracy similar to that of subspecialists; GPT-5-Thinking assistance was also associated with higher generalist diagnostic accuracy. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article.

### 3. 预测利福平耐药结核病患者对氟喹诺酮类药物的耐药性：一项跨国验证研究。

- **英文标题**: Predicting resistance to fluoroquinolones among patients with rifampicin-resistant tuberculosis: A cross-country validation study.
- **期刊**: PLoS medicine (2026)
- **作者**: Tianfang Shao, Mariana R Neves, Molly Franke
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42743308/

**中文摘要**: 氟喹诺酮类（FQs）是世界卫生组织认可的、用于治疗耐利福平或耐多药结核病（RR/MDR-TB）的大多数全口服、较短疗程方案的基石。了解FQs耐药情况有助于在诊疗现场指导方案选择。在无法进行FQ耐药快速检测的环境中，预测模型可通过识别诊疗现场可观察到的患者特征来判断FQ耐药，从而支持治疗决策。这些预测模型通常是在单一国家内开发和评估的，其在不同地理环境中的可推广性尚不明确。我们使用了2012年至2024年间提交至TB Portals的5,175例RR-TB且具有可用FQ药物敏感性试验（DST）结果的患者数据；TB Portals是由美国国家过敏症和传染病研究所管理的开放获取数据共享平台，数据来自八个国家（阿塞拜疆、白俄罗斯、格鲁吉亚、哈萨克斯坦、吉尔吉斯斯坦、摩尔多瓦、罗马尼亚和乌克兰）。在这些患者中，1,772例（34.2%）为FQ耐药结核。我们使用逻辑回归、神经网络和XGBoost开发了FQ耐药预测模型。模型在三种策略下进行评估：（1）基于多国数据训练的汇总模型；（2）采用内部验证进行训练和评估的国家内模型；（3）在部分国家子集上训练，并在留出国家中进行外部验证的跨国模型。模型区分度通过受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUROC）和精确率-召回率曲线下面积（AUPRC）评估。在三种算法中，汇总模型显示出中等程度的经乐观校正区分度，AUROC范围为0.70至0.72，AUPRC范围为0.57至0.59。国家内模型表现出略好的区分度，部分国家的AUROC和AUPRC达到0.8。跨国外部验证显示，使用外部数据训练的模型所导致的性能损失可能可忽略，也可能大于0.1 AUPROC或AUROC，取决于国家和算法。一小组预测因子，包括病例定义和治疗史相关变量，是最一致具有信息量的预测因子，而人口学、合并症、社会风险、教育和就业变量在不同国家和算法之间的贡献更具变异性。我们研究的一个局限是，分析数据集中RR-TB和FQ耐药的发生率相对稳定。因此，结果可能无法推广至MDR-TB动态发生显著变化的情形。使用人口学和临床特征预测FQ耐药，在识别RR-TB患者中的FQ耐药方面显示出中等能力，但其性能和预测因子模式在各国间存在差异。为一个或几个国家开发的模型，若未经严格的外部验证，不能假定可推广至其他环境。这些发现凸显了全球训练的RR/MDR-TB预测模型的局限性，并强调需要基于本地信息的预测模型来支持临床决策。

**英文摘要**: Fluoroquinolones (FQs) are a cornerstone of most all-oral, shorter regimens endorsed by the World Health Organization for the treatment of rifampicin-resistant or multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (RR/MDR-TB). Knowledge of resistance to FQs can help guide regimen selection at the point of care. In settings where rapid testing for FQ resistance is unavailable, prediction models could support treatment decisions by identifying FQ resistance based on patient characteristics observable at the point of care. These prediction models have been typically developed and evaluated within a single country, and their generalizability across different geographic settings is unclear. We used data from 5,175 patients with RR-TB and available FQ drug susceptibility testing (DST) results submitted to the TB Portals, an open-access data-sharing platform curated by the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, from eight countries (Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Romania and Ukraine) between 2012 and 2024. Among these patients, 1,772 (34.2%) had FQ-resistant TB. We developed prediction models for FQ resistance using logistic regression, neural networks, and XGBoost. Models were evaluated under three strategies: (1) pooled models trained on multi-country data; (2) within-country models trained and evaluated using internal validation; and (3) cross-country models trained on subsets of countries and externally validated on held-out countries. Model discrimination was assessed using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) and the area under the precision-recall curve (AUPRC). Across the three algorithms, pooled models showed moderate optimism-corrected discrimination, with AUROC ranging from 0.70 to 0.72 and AUPRC ranging from 0.57 to 0.59. Within-country models demonstrated slightly better discrimination, with AUROC and AUPRC reaching 0.8 for some countries. Cross-country external validation showed that performance loss from using a model trained on external data could be negligible to >0.1 AUPROC or AUROC, depending on the country and algorithm. A limited set of predictors, including case definition and treatment-history-related variables, were among the most consistently informative predictors, whereas demographic, comorbidity, social-risk, education, and employment variables showed more variable contributions across countries and algorithms. A limitation of our study is that the incidence of RR-TB and FQ resistance was relatively stable in our analysis dataset. Hence, the results may not generalize to scenarios with marked changes in MDR-TB dynamics. Predicting FQ resistance using demographic and clinical characteristics showed moderate ability to identify FQ resistance among patients with RR-TB, but their performance and predictor patterns varied across countries. Models developed for one or several countries cannot be assumed to generalize to other settings without rigorous external validation. These findings highlight the limitations of globally trained prediction models for RR/MDR-TB and underscore the need for locally informed prediction models to support clinical decision-making.
