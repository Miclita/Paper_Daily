### 1. 间歇性气液重排调控多孔介质中的气体混合。

- **英文标题**: Intermittent gas-liquid rearrangement controls the mixing of gases in porous media.
- **期刊**: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America (2026)
- **作者**: Samuel J Jackson, Sharon Ellman, G&#xfc;lce Kalyoncu
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987001/

**中文摘要**: 多孔介质中的气体混合控制着重要的环境和工业应用。在这些应用中，多种气体组分与液态水共享可用孔隙空间，同时发生气体混合和气液再分布。目前，该过程由忽略孔隙尺度动力学的宏观模型来表征。我们证明这种做法并非普遍有效，并可能导致混合速率出现较大误差。我们利用四维X射线成像研究向饱和氪和水的多孔砂岩中注入氮气的过程，同时成像气体混合物组成演化以及气水多相流。与常规对流-扩散模型平滑、连续的表征不同，我们的结果表明，气体混合通过一系列由气体连通性控制的不同阶段演化。最初，非混相气水驱替会产生气体组成方差，因为不同气体占据由水隔开的相邻孔隙。随着气体连通性增加，间歇性的气液界面重排迅速连接具有对比性气体组成的区域，驱动组成方差快速衰减。一旦连通性稳定，混合便由准静态气体网络内的受限扩散过程控制。在后期，进一步的混合由多组分奥斯特瓦尔德熟化缓慢驱动，即滞留气体簇通过周围液相交换溶质。这些发现为多孔介质中的气体混合提供了新认识，并为将孔隙尺度多相流在地下气体混合中的作用进行升尺度表征提供了概念思路。

**英文摘要**: Gas mixing in porous media governs important environmental and industrial applications in which multiple gas components share the available pore space with liquid water, simultaneously undergoing gas mixing and gas-liquid redistribution. Currently, this process is represented by macroscopic models that ignore pore scale dynamics. We demonstrate that this is not generally valid, and can lead to large errors in the mixing rate. We use 4D X-ray imaging to investigate nitrogen injection into a porous sandstone saturated with krypton and water, jointly imaging the gas mixture composition evolution and multiphase flow of gas and water. In contrast to the smooth, continuous representation of the conventional advection-diffusion model, our results show that gas mixing evolves through a sequence of distinct regimes controlled by gas connectivity. Initially, immiscible gas-water displacement generates gas composition variance as the different gases occupy neighboring pores separated by water. As gas connectivity increases, intermittent gas-liquid interfacial rearrangements rapidly connect regions of contrasting gas composition, driving a fast decay in composition variance. Once connectivity stabilizes, mixing is controlled by a confined diffusion process within the quasi-static gas network. At later times, further mixing is driven slowly by multicomponent Ostwald ripening, whereby trapped gas ganglia exchange solutes through the surrounding liquid phase. These findings provide insights into gas mixing in porous media, suggesting concepts for upscaling the role of pore scale multiphase flow in gas mixing in the subsurface.

### 2. 两种深度学习模型及其组合在两个筛查队列中用于1年肺癌检测的比较

- **英文标题**: Comparison of Two Deep Learning Models and Their Combination for 1-year Lung Cancer Detection in Two Screening Cohorts.
- **期刊**: Radiology. Cardiothoracic imaging (2026)
- **作者**: Anthony P Reeves, Mohammad Mozafary, Ali Hashemi
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留

**中文摘要**: **目的** 评估Sybil、一种基于医学人工智能开放网络（MONAI）的结节模型以及二者组合在两个筛查队列——国家肺癌筛查试验（NLST）和潘普洛纳国际早期肺癌行动计划（P-IELCAP）——中1年肺癌检测的性能。

**材料与方法** 分析NL

**英文摘要**: Purpose To evaluate the performance of Sybil, a Medical Open Network for Artificial Intelligence (MONAI)-based nodule model, and their combination for 1-year lung cancer detection in two screening cohorts: the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST) and the Pamplona International Early Lung Cancer Action Program (P-IELCAP). Materials and Methods Baseline low-dose CT (LDCT) examinations from the NLST and the P-IELCAP were analyzed. A prespecified OR-stacked composite model combining Sybil, a MONAI-based nodule model, and a safety-net rule (MONAI-detected nodule > 6 mm with detection confidence > 0.95) was developed and evaluated for 1-year lung cancer detection. Model discrimination was assessed by using area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC), and operating-point performance metrics were calculated. Results A total of 5110 baseline LDCT examinations were analyzed, including 2343 from NLST and 2767 from P-IELCAP, with 65 lung cancers diagnosed within 1 year. Mean age, mean pack-years, and percentage of female participants were 61.6 years ± 5.1 (SD), 57.3 pack-years ± 24.8, and 39.4% (923 of 2343), respectively, in NLST and 56.0 years ± 9.5, 34.4 pack-years ± 21.5, and 28.6% (791 of 2767), respectively, in P-IELCAP. Lung cancer incidence (per 1000 person-years) was higher in NLST than in P-IELCAP (17.7 vs 8.7; P = .006). Mean Sybil 1-year lung cancer probabilities (scaled by 1000 for presentation) were also higher in NLST than in P-IELCAP (16.4 vs 9.9; P < .01). Sybil achieved an AUC of 0.93 in both cohorts, whereas the MONAI-based model achieved AUCs of 0.90 in NLST and 0.79 in P-IELCAP. The composite model missed two of 65 1-year lung cancers while correctly classifying 2731 of 4965 (55.0%) noncancer examinations as negative, yielding a sensitivity of 63 of 65 (96.9%) and a positive predictive value of 63 of 2297 (2.7%). Conclusion For 1-year lung cancer detection with LDCT, the composite AI model achieved high sensitivity while maintaining acceptable specificity.
