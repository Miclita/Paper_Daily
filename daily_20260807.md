**中文摘要**: 许多重要的生物学功能并非源自单个基因，而是由整个基因组编码的复杂相互作用所产生。我们报道了利用基因组语言模型对完整噬菌体基因组进行生成式设计的首次尝试。我们以噬菌体ΦX174为设计模板，生成了具有目标宿主嗜性的可行噬菌体。实验测试产出了16种在实验室条件下具有不同适应性特征的噬菌体。冷冻电子显微镜证实，一种生成的噬菌体在其衣壳中利用了一种进化上相距甚远的DNA包装蛋白。由生成的噬菌体组成的混合物能够迅速克服对ΦX174具有抗性的大肠杆菌菌株，这展示了人工智能生成的噬菌体疗法对抗快速进化细菌病原体的可行路径。这项工作为设计多样化的合成噬菌体及基因组尺度的有用生物系统提供了蓝图。

**英文摘要**: Many important biological functions arise not from single genes but from complex interactions encoded by entire genomes. We report the first generative design of complete bacteriophage genomes using genome language models. We generated viable bacteriophages with target host tropism, using the phage ΦX174 as our design template. Experimental testing yielded 16 phages with diverse fitness profiles in laboratory conditions. Cryo-electron microscopy confirmed that a generated phage utilizes an evolutionarily distant DNA packaging protein in its capsid. A cocktail of generated phages rapidly overcomes ΦX174-resistant Escherichia coli strains, demonstrating a path toward artificial intelligence-generated phage therapies against rapidly evolving bacterial pathogens. This work provides a blueprint for the design of diverse synthetic bacteriophages and useful biological systems at the genome scale.

**中文摘要**: 目的：评估基于人工智能（AI）从冠状动脉钙化（CAC）CT中获取的心腔容积测量是否能够改善心力衰竭（HF）风险预测。

材料与方法：本回顾性研究纳入2010年至2023年间接受CAC CT检查且无已知心脏病的无症状患者。使用经过验证的AI模型分析CAC CT图像，以计算心腔容积。HF事件根据国际疾病分类第九版和第十版编码进行识别。采用Cox比例风险回归模型，纳入心腔容积并校正心血管疾病事件预测风险-心力衰竭（PREVENT-HF）评分，评估其与HF的关联。计算3年、5年、8年和10年的时间依赖性受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC），以比较容积测量、PREVENT-HF、CAC评分及其联合模型的预测性能。

结果：共纳入5892例患者（平均年龄±标准差，58.2岁±9.4；男性3258例）。在平均4年±3的随访期间，377例患者（6.3%）发生HF。较大的左心房、左心室、右心房容积及左心室心肌容积与HF风险增加相关（P < .001）。包含所有心腔容积、CAC评分和PREVENT-HF评分的复合多变量Cox回归模型在预测10年HF风险方面优于单独使用PREVENT-HF（AUC，0.80 vs 0.76；ΔAUC，0.04；P < .001）和单独使用CAC评分（AUC，0.80 vs 0.70；ΔAUC，0.10；P < .001）。研究还发现存在时相-容积交互效应，表明在校正PREVENT-HF后，舒张期容积与收缩期容积相比与更高的HF风险独立相关（P < .001）。

结论：与单独使用PREVENT-HF或CAC评分相比，基于CAC CT获得的AI心腔容积测量改善了HF风险预测。

**英文摘要**: Purpose To evaluate whether artificial intelligence (AI)-derived chamber volumetry from coronary artery calcium (CAC) CT improves heart failure (HF) risk prediction. Materials and Methods This retrospective study included asymptomatic patients without known cardiac disease undergoing CAC CT between 2010 and 2023. CAC CT images were analyzed using a validated AI model to calculate chamber volumes. HF events were identified based on International Classification of Diseases, Ninth and Tenth Revisions codes. Cox proportional hazard regression models incorporating chamber volumes, adjusted for Predicting Risk of cardiovascular disease EVENTs-Heart Failure (PREVENT-HF) score, were used to assess the association with HF. Time-dependent areas under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUCs) at 3, 5, 8, and 10 years were calculated to compare the performance of volumetry, PREVENT-HF, CAC scoring, and their combination. Results A total of 5892 patients were included (mean age ± SD, 58.2 years ± 9.4; 3258 male). During a mean follow-up of 4 years ± 3, 377 patients (6.3%) developed HF. Larger left atrial, left ventricular, right atrial, and left ventricular myocardial volumes were associated with increased HF risk ( P < .001). The composite, multivariable Cox regression model incorporating all chamber volumes, CAC score, and PREVENT-HF scores outperformed PREVENT-HF (AUC, 0.80 vs 0.76; ΔAUC, 0.04; P < .001) and CAC scores (AUC, 0.80 vs 0.70; ΔAUC, 0.10; P < .001) alone in predicting 10-year HF risk. A phase-volume interaction effect was identified, indicating that diastolic volumes were independently associated with higher HF risk than were systolic volumes ( P < .001), adjusted for PREVENT-HF. Conclusion AI-derived cardiac chamber volumetry obtained from CAC CT improved HF risk prediction compared with PREVENT-HF or CAC scores alone.
