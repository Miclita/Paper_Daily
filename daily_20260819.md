### 1. 行动呼吁：加速人工智能驱动的医学影像及更广泛医疗保健系统变革

- **英文标题**: Call to Action: Accelerating AI-driven Transformation in Medical Imaging and the Broader Health Care System.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Linda Moy, Alberto Vargas, Judy Wawira Gichoya
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 8/10 — 文章聚焦医学影像AI转型与医疗系统变革，高度契合医学影像AI方向，但属观点呼吁类而非原创研究。
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42610812/

**中文摘要**: 全球影像学需求现已超出承载能力，其原因在于人口老龄化、合并症增多、影像学适应症扩大以及劳动力短缺。这些压力正在重塑各国的医疗卫生政策。与此同时，扩大影像服务可及性与降低成本之间的竞争性需求，正推动人们通过整合人工智能（AI）等变革性技术来重新构想影像服务的提供方式。本共识声明由国际放射学战略研究学会（IS3R）发布，是一份行动号召，概述了具有前景的人工智能解决方案，这些方案能够提高工作流程效率，并使放射学与卫生系统和支付方的优先事项保持一致。本声明反映了2025年8月在爱尔兰都柏林举行的IS3R会议的专家共识，在数字化加速、快速创新和数据驱动的患者护理背景下，为放射学确立了战略方向。本文涵盖四个领域：图像判读、能力建设、数据治理和系统性数字化转型。本文探讨了人工智能如何应对不断增长的影像需求和成本，同时解决与提高数据质量、标准化和互操作性相关的挑战。文章还强调了重新设计工作流程和适应新型护理模式的必要性，同时保持高质量服务。最后，本文提出了共识性建议，概述了人工智能如何实现多模态健康数据整合，并推动与价值导向型医疗的对接。

**英文摘要**: Global imaging demand now exceeds capacity due to an aging population, rising comorbidities, expanded indications for imaging, and workforce shortages. These pressures are reshaping national health care policy. At the same time, the competing demands to expand access to imaging services while reducing costs are creating momentum to reimagine imaging delivery through the integration of transformative technologies like artificial intelligence (AI). This consensus statement from the International Society for Strategic Studies in Radiology (IS3R) is a call to action, outlining promising AI solutions that increase workflow efficiency and align radiology with health system and payer priorities. Reflecting expert consensus from the IS3R meeting in Dublin, Ireland, in August 2025, this statement defines strategic directions for radiology amid accelerating digitalization, rapid innovation, and data-driven patient care. This article covers four domains: image interpretation, capacity building, data governance, and systemic digital transformation. This article examines how AI can address rising imaging demand and costs while addressing challenges related to improving data quality, standardization, and interoperability. It also highlights the need to re-engineer workflows and adapt to new care models while maintaining high-quality services. Finally, it presents consensus recommendations outlining how AI can enable multimodal health data integration and advance alignment with value-based care.

### 2. 大语言模型驱动的急性肾损伤多中心预测与可解释风险归因

- **英文标题**: Large language model driven multicenter prediction and explainable risk attribution of acute kidney injury.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Lingyi Xu, Kun Yan, Zinuo Zhang
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42608399/

**中文摘要**: 急性肾损伤（AKI）是住院患者中的一种危及生命的状况，早期预测可实现有效预防。尽管现有模型已取得进展，但其临床应用仍受制于过高的假阳性率（70%-94%）以及缺乏可操作的临床见解。我们开展了一项多中心回顾性队列研究，并开发了一个双模型大语言模型框架：AKI-PM（预测模型）用于预测24小时内AKI的发生，AKI-RAM（风险归因模型）用于提供可解释的风险归因。利用来自中国四家不同地理区域医院的140,637例住院病例队列，我们证明AKI-PM在内部验证中取得了较高的预测性能（曲线下面积0.95，阳性预测值0.68），并在少样本迁移后在外部站点保持了稳健的泛化能力（曲线下面积0.92-0.96，阳性预测值0.69-0.74）。关键在于，AKI-RAM通过区分可改变与不可改变的危险因素并提供个性化建议，能够提供结构化且具有临床可操作性的风险解释。在由六名肾脏病专家对来自四家独立医院的200例病例进行的临床评估中，AKI-RAM在八个维度上均获得高分（李克特量表：4.18-4.88），且评估者间信度为中度至良好（组内相关系数：0.680-0.803）。该集成框架通过将准确性与可解释性相结合，解决了人工智能驱动临床预测中的关键局限性，为多样化医疗环境下的早期AKI预防提供了一种可扩展的解决方案。

**英文摘要**: Acute kidney injury (AKI) represents a life-threatening condition among hospitalized patients, where early prediction enables prevention. Despite advances in existing models, clinical implementation remains hindered by excessive false positive rates (70%-94%) and lack of actionable clinical insights. We conduct a multi-center retrospective cohort study and develop a two-model large language model framework: AKI-PM (Prediction Model) for predicting AKI occurrence within 24 hours and AKI-RAM (Risk Attribution Model) for providing explainable risk attribution. Using a cohort of 140,637 hospital admissions across four geographically diverse Chinese hospitals, we demonstrate that AKI-PM achieves high predictive performance in internal validation (area under curve 0.95, positive predictive value 0.68) and maintains robust generalizability across external sites after few-shot (area under curve 0.92-0.96, positive predictive value 0.69-0.74). Crucially, AKI-RAM provides structured, clinically actionable risk explanations by distinguishing modifiable from non-modifiable factors and offering tailored recommendations. In a clinical evaluation of 200 cases from four independent hospitals by six nephrologists, AKI-RAM receives high scores across eight dimensions (Likert scale: 4.18-4.88) with moderate to good inter-rater reliability (intraclass correlation coefficients: 0.680-0.803). This integrated framework addresses critical limitations in AI-driven clinical prediction by combining accuracy with interpretability, offering a scalable solution for early AKI prevention in diverse healthcare settings.

### 3. 基于X线摄影的AI工具用于四肢骨折检测的多国验证及相应阅片者表现。

- **英文标题**: Multinational Validation of a Radiography-Based AI Tool for Appendicular Fracture Detection and Corresponding Reader Performance.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Huibert C Ruitenbeek, Mathias Willadsen Brejneb&#xf8;l, Gozde Gunes
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42610815/

**中文摘要**: 背景 人工智能（AI）骨折检测模型在放射学工作流程中展现出提升诊断效率和准确性的潜力。然而，尽管不同医疗系统和环境下的影像采集方案及临床工作流程可能存在差异，但这些模型在不同国家的性能表现及其对阅片者诊断能力的影响仍未得到充分探索。目的 评估一个预训练的骨折检测工具在三个欧洲医院中的性能，并评估其对临床医生诊断能力的影响。材料与方法 在这项回顾性、多阅片者、多病例交叉研究中，分析了2018年4月至2022年7月期间在三个欧洲医院采集的、年龄在21岁及以上疑似四肢骨折患者的X光片。参考标准由当地专家放射科医师确立。AI工具的性能通过受试者工作特征曲线下面积、敏感性和特异性进行评估。采用χ²检验比较不同医院及不同解剖亚组间的敏感性和特异性，并使用广义估计方程比较不同阅片者间的差异。结果 共纳入1500名患者（平均年龄，43.3岁±19.5 [标准差]；820名男性）。AI工具的医院级敏感性范围为500例中的400例（80%；95%置信区间：76，84）到500例中的430例（86%；95%置信区间：83，90），特异性范围为500例中的465例（93%；95%置信区间：91，95）到500例中的480例（96%；95%置信区间：95，98）。基于解剖部位的亚组分析显示，在一所医院中，腕/手和手指检查的敏感性较低，为140例中的105例（75%），而另外两所医院分别为138例中的125例（91%）和160例中的150例（94%）（P = .004），但所有医院的整体敏感性或特异性未观察到显著差异（P值分别为 .23 和 .10）。AI工具的使用提高了阅片者的整体敏感性（提高11个百分点 [95%置信区间：6.8，14.4；P < .001]），同时保持了特异性（提高0.6个百分点 [95%置信区间：-0.6，1.7；P = .34]）。结论 各中心间未观察到AI性能的差异，且AI辅助与阅片者敏感性提高相关。© 作者 2026年。由北美放射学会根据CC BY 4.0许可发布。本文提供补充材料。另见本期Maas和Beekman的社论。

**英文摘要**: Background Artificial intelligence (AI) models for fracture detection have shown promise in improving diagnostic efficiency and accuracy in radiology workflows. However, despite potential differences in imaging protocols and clinical workflows in different health care systems and settings, the performance across countries of these models and their effects on reader performance remain underexplored. Purpose To evaluate the performance of a pretrained fracture detection tool across three European hospitals and assess its impact on clinician performance. Materials and Methods In this retrospective, multireader, multicase crossover study, the radiographs of patients aged 21 years or older with suspected appendicular fractures acquired from April 2018 to July 2022, across three European hospitals, were analyzed. Reference standards were established by expert local radiologists. AI tool performance was evaluated with the area under the receiver operator characteristic curve, sensitivity, and specificity. Sensitivity and specificity were statistically compared across hospitals and per-anatomy subgroup with the χ 2 test and across readers with generalized estimating equations. Results A total of 1500 patients (mean age, 43.3 years ± 19.5 [SD]; 820 men) were included. The hospital-level sensitivities of the AI tool ranged from 400 of 500 (80%; 95% CI: 76, 84) to 430 of 500 (86%; 95% CI: 83, 90) and specificities ranged from 465 of 500 (93%; 95% CI: 91, 95) to 480 of 500 (96%; 95% CI: 95, 98). Anatomy-based subgroup analysis revealed lower sensitivity of 105 of 140 (75%) for wrist/hand and finger examinations in one hospital versus 125 of 138 (91%) and 150 of 160 (94%) ( P = .004), but no differences in overall sensitivity or specificity were observed across all hospitals ( P = .23 and P = .10, respectively). Use of the AI tool improved overall reader sensitivity (11 percentage points [95% CI: 6.8, 14.4; P < .001]) while maintaining specificity (0.6 percentage points [95% CI: -0.6, 1.7; P = .34]). Conclusion No differences in AI performance were observed across centers, and AI assistance was associated with higher reader sensitivity. © The Author(s) 2026. ### 4. 诊断校准的AI图像质量框架在MRI前列腺癌检测中的应用

- **英文标题**: Diagnostically Calibrated AI Image Quality Framework Applied to Prostate Cancer Detection at MRI.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Tiago F Coelho, Joeran S Bosma, Vilma Bozgo
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42610819/

**中文摘要**: **背景** 图像质量影响诊断性能；然而，当前的质量指标依赖于主观的阅片者标注，而非诊断结果。

**目的** 开发并评估一种使用深度学习（DL）的诊断校准型人工智能（AI）框架，用于图像质量评估，将前列腺MRI质量评分与诊断性能相关联。

**材料与方法** 这项单中心回顾性研究分析了2014年1月至2023年12月期间在拉德堡德大学医学中心连续进行的12,496次前列腺多参数MRI检查。实施了以下两步框架：首先，在最具可靠性的低质量（受伪影影响而退化的结构）和高质量（边界清晰分区）的轴向T2加权图像上训练DL模型，以产生连续图像质量评分。其次，将该评分应用于独立的内部测试集，并与诊断性能进行校准，诊断性能以（a）AI模型检测临床显著性前列腺癌（csPCa）的受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC）和（b）放射科医师在常规临床实践中检测csPCa的准确性来衡量。参考标准为活检的组织病理学结果。计算质量评分从10%至80%的十分位阈值所对应的诊断性能。使用对回归斜率的置换检验评估性能与阈值之间的线性趋势。

**结果** 共使用1,229个T2加权序列进行训练，568次多参数MRI检查用于诊断测试（1,638名男性患者；中位年龄67岁[IQR，62–71岁]）。在五折交叉验证中，DL模型在训练集中区分低质量与高质量图像的平均AUC为0.99。在测试集中检测csPCa时，AI模型的AUC从0.92（95% CI：0.89，0.94）提升至0.99（95% CI：0.97，1.00）（P = .005），跨越DL模型图像质量评分的各阈值；放射科医师的准确性从77%（95% CI：73，80）提升至85%（95% CI：78，91）（P = .01）。

**结论** AI框架提供了一种标准化方法，可获得客观的图像质量评分，该评分与AI模型诊断性能的提升及放射科医师准确性的提高相关。

**英文摘要**: Background Image quality affects diagnostic performance; however, current quality metrics rely on subjective reader labels rather than diagnostic outcomes. Purpose To develop and evaluate a diagnostically calibrated artificial intelligence (AI) framework using deep learning (DL) for image quality assessment, linking prostate MRI quality scores to diagnostic performance. Materials and Methods This single-center retrospective study analyzed 12 496 consecutive prostate multiparametric MRI examinations performed between January 2014 and December 2023 at Radboud University Medical Center. A two-step framework was implemented: First, a DL model was trained on the most reliable low-quality (artifact-degraded structures) and high-quality (clear delineated zones) axial T2-weighted images to produce a continuous image quality score. Second, this score was applied in an independent internal test set and calibrated with diagnostic performance measured as (a) area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) for clinically significant prostate cancer (csPCa) detection by an AI model and (b) accuracy of csPCa detection by radiologists in routine clinical practice. The reference standard was histopathologic findings at biopsy. Diagnostic performance was calculated for quality score 10-percentile thresholds from 10% to 80%. Linear trend between performance and threshold was assessed using a permutation test on the regression slope. Results A total of 1229 T2-weighted series were used for training, and 568 multiparametric MRI examinations for diagnostic testing (1638 male patients; median age, 67 years [IQR, 62-71 years]). The DL model achieved a mean AUC of 0.99 for distinguishing low- versus high-quality images in the training set during fivefold cross-validation. For csPCa detection in the test set, the AI model AUC improved from 0.92 (95% CI: 0.89, 0.94) to 0.99 (95% CI: 0.97, 1.00) ( P = .005) across thresholds of DL model image quality score; radiologist accuracy improved from 77% (95% CI: 73, 80) to 85% (95% CI: 78, 91) ( P = .01). Conclusion The AI framework provided a standardized method to derive an objective image quality score that correlated with improved AI model diagnostic performance and radiologist accuracy. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article.
