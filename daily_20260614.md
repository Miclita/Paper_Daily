### 1. 通用大型语言模型在医学基准测试中优于专门的临床AI工具。

- **英文标题**: General-purpose large language models outperform specialized clinical AI tools on medical benchmarks.
- **期刊**: Nature medicine (2026)
- **作者**: Krithik Vishwanath, Anton Alyakin, Mrigayu Ghosh
- **类别**: LLM 临床应用 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42286322/

**中文摘要**: 专门的临床人工智能（AI）工具正在进入医疗实践，尽管其缺乏独立的评估。我们对两款基于大型语言模型（LLM）构建的临床AI工具——OpenEvidence和UpToDate Expert AI——与三款前沿LLM进行了定量比较：GPT-5.2、Gemini 3.1 Pro和Claude Opus 4.6。我们的评估分为三个阶段：（1）500道MedQA问题测试医学知识；（2）500项HealthBench项目衡量与临床医生的一致性；（3）真实临床查询（RCQ）基准，该基准由医生在真实临床环境中向通用语言模型提出的100个去标识查询构建而成。对于RCQ基准，12名美国临床医生对模型输出进行了随机、盲法评审，产生了1800条模型-问题注释。在所有三项评估中，前沿LLM均优于临床AI工具。在RCQ上，临床AI工具的表现与自动启用的Google Search AI Overview相当。这些发现凸显了在AI工具进入临床环境之前对其进行独立、真实世界评估的必要性。

**英文摘要**: Specialized clinical artificial intelligence (AI) tools are entering medical practice despite scarce independent evaluation. We quantitatively evaluate two clinical AI tools, OpenEvidence and UpToDate Expert AI, built on large language models (LLMs) against three frontier LLMs: GPT-5.2, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Claude Opus 4.6. Our evaluation has three stages: (1) 500 MedQA questions testing medical knowledge, (2) 500 HealthBench items measuring alignment with clinicians and (3) the real clinical queries (RCQ) benchmark, built from 100 de-identified queries from physicians to a general-purpose language model in a live clinical environment. For the RCQ benchmark, 12 US clinicians performed randomized, blinded review of model outputs, producing 1,800 model-question annotations. Frontier LLMs outperformed clinical AI tools in all three evaluations. Clinical AI tools performed comparably to auto-enabled Google Search AI Overview on the RCQ. These findings highlight the need for independent, real-world evaluation of AI tools before they enter clinical settings.

### 2. 多模态基础模型利用文本进行医学图像预测。

- **英文标题**: Multimodal foundation models exploit text to make medical image predictions.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Thomas A Buckley, James A Diao, Cam N Srivastava
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42285926/

**中文摘要**: 多模态基础模型在医学影像解读中展现出引人注目但存在矛盾的表现。然而，这些模型如何整合并优先处理包括图像和文本在内的不同数据模态，目前仍知之甚少。我们使用1090个多模态医学案例评估了8个专有和开源的多模态基础模型。研究表明，图像预测在很大程度上由文本驱动，且准确率随着信息性文本量的增加而单调递增。对文本的利用是一把双刃剑：即使文本中存在轻微的错误诊断暗示，也会削弱基于图像的分类能力，导致模型在原本仅凭图像即可正确回答的案例中性能骤降——例如，当引入具有误导性的临床小插曲时，o3模型的准确率从84%降至28%。在医师对长篇病例的评估中，当文本信息高度丰富时，添加图像反而会降低或无法提升模型表现（例如，在69场临床病理会议中，向高度信息性文本添加图像后，GPT-4V的准确率有所下降）。我们的结果表明，多模态AI模型可能在医学诊断推理中具有应用价值，但其准确性在很大程度上（无论好坏）是由文本驱动的。

**英文摘要**: Multimodal foundation models have shown compelling but conflicting performance in medical image interpretation. However, the ways in which these models integrate and prioritize different data modalities, including images and text, remain poorly understood. Here we evaluate 8 proprietary and open-source multimodal foundation models using 1090 multimodal medical cases. We show that image predictions are largely driven by text, with accuracy increasing monotonically with the amount of informative text. Exploitation of text is a double-edged sword; even mild suggestions of an incorrect diagnosis in text diminish image-based classification, dramatically reducing performance in cases the model could previously answer using images alone-o3 accuracy fell from 84% to 28% when a misleading clinical vignette was introduced. In physician evaluations of long-form cases, adding images reduces or does not improve performance when text is highly informative (e.g., GPT-4V showed decreased accuracy when images were added to highly informative text across 69 clinicopathological conferences). Our results suggest that multimodal AI models may be useful in medical diagnostic reasoning but that their accuracy is largely driven, for better and worse, by text.

### 3. 高级视觉区域类似于具有强选择性和功能特化的领域通用滤波器。

- **英文标题**: Higher visual areas act like domain-general filters with strong selectivity and functional specialization.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Meenakshi Khosla, Leila Wehbe
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42285966/

**中文摘要**: 神经科学研究高度依赖于先验假设，这可能导致研究结果偏向现有理论。本文采用了一种与假设无关的方法，研究高级视觉皮层的类别选择性。仅利用刺激图像及其相关的fMRI活动，我们将随机初始化的神经网络约束为预测体素活动。尽管没有类别级别的监督，训练后的网络单元却能充当"面孔"或"词语"等语义概念的检测器，为类别选择性提供了坚实的实证支持。重要的是，当训练网络时排除包含偏好类别的图像，这种选择性大多得以维持，这有力地表明，选择性并非领域特异性机制，而是对表征偏好类别的通用模式的敏感性。模型表征向感知任务迁移的能力进一步揭示了其选择性反应的功能作用。最后，我们的模型仅对有限数量的类别表现出选择性，且这些类别均已被前人识别，提示基本类别已经明确。

**英文摘要**: Neuroscientific studies rely heavily on a-priori hypotheses, which can bias results toward existing theories. Here, we use a hypothesis-neutral approach to study category selectivity in higher visual cortex. Using only stimulus images and their associated fMRI activity, we constrain randomly initialized neural networks to predict voxel activity. Despite no category-level supervision, units in the trained networks act as detectors for semantic concepts like 'faces' or 'words', providing solid empirical support for categorical selectivity. Importantly, this selectivity is mostly maintained when training the networks without images that contain the preferred category, strongly suggesting that selectivity is not domain-specific machinery, but sensitivity to generic patterns that characterize preferred categories. The ability of the models' representations to transfer to perceptual tasks further reveals the functional role of their selective responses. Finally, our models show selectivity only for a limited number of categories, all previously identified, suggesting that the essential categories are already known.
