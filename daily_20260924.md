### 1. 内侧颞叶默认模式网络选择性编码自传体视觉意象。

- **英文标题**: Medial temporal default mode network selectively encodes autobiographical visual imagery.
- **期刊**: Science advances (2026)
- **作者**: Andrew J Anderson, Adam Turnbull, Feng V Lin
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42777057/

**中文摘要**: 人类大脑从记忆中想象视觉场景的能力被认为依赖于默认模式网络的内侧颞子系统（MT-DMN），但支持这一能力的神经编码仍所知甚少。我们将功能磁共振成像（fMRI）与视觉及语言人工智能模型相结合，以刻画自传体想象过程中的神经编码。50名参与者在接受fMRI扫描时，依据通用文本提示（例如婚礼、锻炼和驾驶）想象重新体验20个自然场景。使用Stable Diffusion对各个场景进行建模，以根据事先收集的、对所想象场景的语言描述生成个性化合成图像。这些描绘随后被转换为图像识别网络的隐藏状态表征。表征相似性分析显示，MT-DMN编码了图像表征中参与者特异的表征结构，即使在控制由大型语言模型得到的语义特征后依然如此。该效应在其他脑网络中以及在没有主动想象的阅读过程中均不存在，从而表明MT-DMN是自传体体验视觉重建的核心基础。

**英文摘要**: The human brain's capacity to imagine visual scenes from memory is thought to rely on the medial temporal subsystem of the default mode network (MT-DMN), yet the neural codes supporting this ability remain poorly understood. We combined functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) with vision and language artificial intelligence models to characterize neural codes during autobiographical imagination. Fifty participants imagined reexperiencing 20 natural scenarios while undergoing fMRI when cued by generic text prompts (e.g., wedding, exercising, and driving). Individual scenes were modeled using Stable Diffusion to generate personalized synthetic images from verbal descriptions of the scenarios imagined, collected beforehand. These depictions were then transformed into image recognition network hidden state representations. Representational similarity analysis revealed that the MT-DMN encoded the participant-specific representational structure of image representations, even when controlling for semantic features derived from a large language model. This effect was absent in other brain networks and during reading without active imagination, identifying the MT-DMN as a core substrate for the visual reconstruction of autobiographical experiences.
