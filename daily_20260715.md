### 1. 用于B细胞非霍奇金淋巴瘤分类的可解释人工智能：一项诊断决策支持研究。

- **英文标题**: Self-explaining artificial intelligence for the classification of B cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma: A diagnostic decision support study.
- **期刊**: PLoS medicine (2026)
- **作者**: Michael C Thrun, J&#xf6;rg Hoffmann, Stefan W Krause
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 9/10 — AI直接用于B细胞淋巴瘤的流式细胞术诊断分类，属于医学影像/细胞分析AI诊断决策支持，AI为核心贡献且强调可解释性，完全符合感兴趣方向。
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42441729/

**中文摘要**: 多参数流式细胞术是B细胞非霍奇金淋巴瘤（B-NHL）诊断的基石，但其判读需要丰富的专业知识，且因高维数据、样本质量参差不齐、罕见实体数据有限以及临床分类体系不断更新而变得复杂。当前的人工智能方法通常需要大规模训练数据集，且对单个诊断决策背后的依据提供有限见解。我们开发了FlowXAI，一种自解释性人工智能系统，旨在支持B-NHL分类的同时明确报告病例层面的诊断可信度。FlowXAI将无监督结构分析与基于临床动机、反映常规诊断优先顺序的多层级诊断框架相结合。一种无监督的"Tile挖掘"（TM）流程通过识别结构异常样本进行诊断前样本质量评估。TM用于过滤训练数据，显著降低训练需求，同时保持对独立测试样本的无偏评估。FlowXAI通过19,493例外周血样本的重复交叉验证进行评估，并在另一独立诊断中心使用不同抗体组合生成的独立外部基准数据集上进一步验证。在各诊断层级上，FlowXAI在训练样本需求减少约两个数量级的情况下，实现了与基于深度学习的系统相当的性能。当系统内部自我评估将预测分类为可靠时，其诊断性能超过了神经网络基线水平。无监督结构分析显示，正常对照组与特定淋巴瘤实体（如慢性淋巴细胞白血病样淋巴瘤和毛细胞白血病）之间具有明确分离，而其他实体在使用所研究抗体组合时无法清晰区分。FlowXAI为非标准化流式细胞术数据中的B-NHL免疫表型分析提供了准确、数据高效且透明的支持。通过将可解释的决策逻辑与明确的自我评估相结合，FlowXAI为诊断支持和培训提供了具有临床意义的框架，尤其在专家资源有限或罕见淋巴瘤亚型的情境下。主要局限性在于使用特定抗体组合进行回顾性评估，FlowXAI需作为整合诊断工作流程中的决策支持工具进行前瞻性验证。

**英文摘要**: Multiparameter flow cytometry is a cornerstone of B cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma (B-NHL) diagnostics, but interpretation requires substantial expertise and is complicated by high-dimensional data, variable sample quality, limited data for rare entities, and evolving clinical classification systems. Current artificial intelligence approaches often require large training datasets and provide limited insight into the rationale behind individual diagnostic decisions. We developed FlowXAI, a self-explaining artificial intelligence system designed to support B-NHL classification while explicitly reporting case-level diagnostic trustworthiness. FlowXAI combines unsupervised structural analysis with a clinically motivated, multi-level diagnostic framework reflecting routine diagnostic priorities. An unsupervised Tile Mining (TM) procedure performs pre-diagnostic sample-quality assessment by identifying structurally atypical samples. TM is applied to filter training data, enabling substantial reduction of training requirements while preserving unbiased evaluation on independent test samples. FlowXAI was evaluated using repeated cross-validation on 19,493 peripheral blood samples and further assessed on an independent external benchmark dataset generated at a separate diagnostic center using a different antibody panel. Across diagnostic levels, FlowXAI achieved performance comparable to a deep learning-based system despite requiring approximately two orders of magnitude fewer training samples. When predictions were classified as confident by the system's internal self-assessment, diagnostic performance exceeded that of the neural network baseline. Unsupervised structural analysis demonstrated clear separation between normal controls and selected lymphoma entities such as chronic lymphocytic leukemia-like lymphomas and hairy cell leukemia, while other entities were not clearly separable using the antibody panels studied. FlowXAI provides accurate, data-efficient, and transparent support for B-NHL immunophenotyping from nonstandardized flow cytometry data. By combining interpretable decision logic with explicit self-assessment, FlowXAI offers a clinically meaningful framework for diagnostic support and training, particularly in settings with limited expert availability or rare lymphoma subtypes. The main limitation is the retrospective evaluation using specific antibody panels, and FlowXAI requires prospective validation as a decision-support tool within integrated diagnostic workflows.

### 2. **自动化偏见在行动：有或无AI提示下人类阅读筛查性乳房X光片的眼动追踪研究**

- **英文标题**: Automation Bias in Action: Eye Tracking of Humans Reading Screening Mammograms with and without AI Prompts.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Adnan G Taib, George J W Partridge, Peter Phillips
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 7/10 — 研究医学影像AI辅助下的人类决策偏差（自动化偏差），涉及乳腺X光筛查和眼动追踪，对临床实践有参考价值，但AI非核心算法贡献。
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42446361/

**中文摘要**: 背景：错误的人工智能（AI）建议可能导致自动化偏见；然而，其对医学影像解读的影响尚缺乏充分研究。  
目的：评估错误AI建议如何影响乳腺摄影筛查解读者的诊断准确性、阅读时间及视觉搜索行为。  
材料与方法：本回顾性多阅片者配对研究于2024年9月至2025年2月进行，10名英国国家医疗服务体系乳腺筛查项目（NHS Breast Screening Programme）的乳腺摄影解读员评估了一套双视图乳腺摄影筛查检查的测试集。测试集包括经3年随访或组织病理学分析验证的真阳性（TP）、假阴性（FN）、假阳性（FP）和真阴性（TN）AI建议。第一轮，解读员在无AI辅助下解读病例。6周后的第二轮，使用市售AI工具作为决策支持，显示区域评分≥10分（0-100分制）的提示。眼动追踪摄像头记录解读员对特定图像区域的注视（持续凝视）。采用Wilcoxon符号秩检验进行两轮配对比较，Kruskal-Wallis检验比较不同AI结果病例。  
结果：测试集（n=60）包含26例TP、14例FN、14例FP和6例TN AI建议的病例。与无辅助解读（71%；P=0.002）相比，使用AI解读时，面对FN AI建议病例的解读员中位敏感性较低（39%）。面对FP AI建议病例时，解读员特异性较高（39% vs 21%；P=0.004）。可见性（TP和FP）AI提示数量增加导致中位阅读时间从25秒（0个提示）延长至34秒（≥4个提示）（P=0.001）。与无辅助解读相比，解读员在回顾AI未能检测的癌症病例（FN建议）时注视频率降低（0.44次/秒 vs 0.47次/秒；P=0.03）。与无辅助解读相比，解读员解读FP AI建议病例时注视持续时间缩短（0.54秒 vs 0.56秒；P=0.001）。  
结论：在乳腺摄影解读过程中，错误AI建议同时影响解读员的准确性和视觉搜索行为。FN AI建议带来的负面效应最大；因此，应相应调整AI阈值。

**英文摘要**: Background Incorrect artificial intelligence (AI) suggestions can lead to automation bias; however, their impact on medical image interpretation is underresearched. Purpose To assess how incorrect AI suggestions influence the diagnostic accuracy, read times, and visual search behavior of readers interpreting screening mammograms. Materials and Methods In this retrospective multireader paired study conducted between September 2024 and February 2025, 10 National Health Service Breast Screening Programme mammography readers evaluated a test set of two-view mammography screening examinations. The test set included true-positive (TP), false-negative (FN), false-positive (FP), and true-negative (TN) AI suggestions, verified by 3 years of follow-up or histopathologic analysis. In round 1, readers interpreted cases without AI. In round 2, conducted 6 weeks later, a commercially available AI tool was used as decision support, displaying prompts with a region score of 10 or higher (scale, 0-100). Eye-tracking cameras recorded readers' fixations-maintained gaze-over specific image areas. Wilcoxon signed rank tests were used for paired comparisons between rounds, and Kruskal-Wallis tests compared cases with different AI outcomes. Results The test set ( n = 60) included cases with 26 TP, 14 FN, 14 FP, and six TN AI suggestions. Median reader sensitivity was lower for cases with FN AI suggestions when reading cases with AI (39%) compared with unassisted reading (71%; P = .002). Reader specificity was higher for cases with FP AI suggestions (39% vs 21%; P = .004). A greater number of visible (TP and FP) AI prompts led to longer median read times, from 25 seconds (zero prompts) to 34 seconds (four or more prompts) ( P = .001). Readers fixated less when reviewing cancer cases that AI failed to detect (FN suggestions) compared with unassisted reading (0.44 vs 0.47 fixations per second; P = .03). Shorter fixation durations were observed when readers interpreted cases with FP AI suggestions compared with unassisted reading (0.54 vs 0.56 second; P = .001). Conclusion Incorrect AI suggestions influenced both reader accuracy and visual search behaviors during mammography interpretation. The greatest negative impact was observed with FN AI suggestions; therefore, AI thresholds should be calibrated accordingly. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article.
