### 1. 基于尿液SERS光谱和颈动脉B超的缺血性中风双模式分析

- **英文标题**: Dual-mode analysis of ischemic stroke based on urine SERS spectra and carotid B-ultrasound.
- **期刊**: Science advances (2026)
- **作者**: Wenrou Yu, Zhaoxian Chen, Yao Liu
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42384817/

**中文摘要**: 实现对缺血性卒中（IS）的无创高频监测仍是临床及时干预和精准二级预防的主要挑战。建立患者全身微观分子指纹与局部宏观器官病理事件之间的精确关联，对于克服单模态检测的局限性并提升临床风险评估效能至关重要。然而，由于异质性数据的复杂性，如何有效整合跨维度的“分子成像”数据仍是实现这一目标的关键瓶颈。在此，我们提出一种概念验证方法，用于区分确诊IS患者与健康对照（HC），该方法基于机器学习（ML）对尿液表面增强拉曼光谱（SERS）（一维）和颈动脉B超成像（CBI）（二维）进行分析。在包含101名参与者的探索性队列中，该方法分析了10100条SERS光谱和481张CBI图像，实现了92%的分类准确率和0.95的曲线下面积（AUC）。此外，通过结合SERS光谱与液相色谱-质谱联用技术，本研究初步探索了HC/IS组间的尿液生物标志物差异。本研究提出的多维数据融合策略，通过系统关联微流体生物标志物与宏观器官成像特征，有效弥合了传统分子检测与临床表型之间的信息鸿沟。该方法为HC/IS组分类中的风险分层和临床决策提供了一种此前未被探索的无创、高精度工具。

**英文摘要**: Achieving noninvasive high-frequency monitoring of ischemic stroke (IS) remains a major clinical challenge for timely intervention and precise secondary prevention. Establishing precise correlations between patients' systemic microscopic molecular fingerprints and localized macroscopic organ pathological events is essential to overcome the limitations of single-modal detection and enhance the efficacy of clinical risk assessment. However, because of the complexity of heterogeneous data, effectively integrating the cross-dimensional "molecular imaging" data remains a critical bottleneck in achieving this goal. Here, we present a proof-of-concept method to distinguish patients with confirmed IS from healthy controls (HCs) that used machine learning (ML)-based methods to surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) of urine (one-dimensional) and carotid artery B-ultrasound imaging (CBI) (two-dimensional). In an exploratory cohort of 101 participants, this approach analyzed 10,100 SERS spectra and 481 CBI images, achieving 92% classification accuracy and an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.95. Furthermore, by combined SERS spectra and liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry technology, this study preliminarily explored the urinary biomarker differences between HC/IS groups. The multidimensional data fusion strategy proposed in this study effectively bridges the information gap between traditional molecular detection and clinical phenotypes by systematically correlating microfluidic biomarkers with macro-organ imaging features. This approach provides a previously unexplored, noninvasive, and highly accurate tool for risk stratification and clinical decision-making in classifying HC/IS groups.

### 2. 基于案例的AI智能体在血液恶性肿瘤临床决策支持中的应用

- **英文标题**: Clinical decision support in hematological malignancies using a case-grounded AI agent.
- **期刊**: Nature medicine (2026)
- **作者**: Julian Zoller, Michael Kalz, Xuewei Wu
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42380678/

**中文摘要**: 多学科肿瘤委员会整合纵向治疗史、分子分型及快速更新的循证证据，以指导血液系统恶性肿瘤的决策制定，但获取这种亚专科审议资源的途径日益不均衡。本文开发了HemaGuide——一种可本地部署的模块化大语言模型代理，能够将非结构化临床文档转化为结构化病例表征，自主将病例路由至特定决策模式（'指南'、'进阶'与'分子'），并将治疗建议锚定于疾病特异性指南流程图及包含超过2000例真实肿瘤委员会病例的临床决策记忆库。在基于六种基础模型的45例高复杂度病例专家盲法基准测试中，HemaGuide与肿瘤委员会决策的一致性显著提升。针对11个层级的系统性消融实验证实，性能提升具有路由类型依赖性，且没有任何单一组件足以应对所有病例类型。对70例临床相关错义变异的自动分类显示其与专家标准高度一致：未发生将致瘤变异降级为良性变异的情况，且整个工作流程在商品化硬件上以实时条件完成，中位延迟为39秒，而传统人工分子委员会工作流程通常需要数小时。在模拟实践研究中，代理辅助住院医师达到了接近资深医师的一致性水平，并在其亚专科领域部分超越资深医师。基于第二学术中心555例独立病例的外部验证在47个病种中实现81.8%的一致性；针对连续64例未经筛选病例的前瞻性1个月静默试验则达到82.8%的一致性。幻觉出现在664例评估病例中的2例（0.3%）。这些证据共同表明：可本地部署、基于病例的大语言模型代理能够在血液系统恶性肿瘤领域提供可审计的临床决策支持，在跨机构及商品化硬件实时条件下均能维持一致性。

**英文摘要**: Multidisciplinary tumor boards integrate longitudinal treatment histories, molecular profiling and rapidly evolving evidence to guide decisions in hematological malignancies, yet access to this level of subspecialty deliberation is increasingly uneven. Here we develop HemaGuide, a locally deployable, modular large language model agent that converts unstructured clinical documents into structured case representations, autonomously routes cases to specialized decision modes ('guideline', 'advanced' and 'molecular') and grounds recommendations in disease-specific guideline flowcharts and a clinical decision memory of >2,000 real-world tumor board cases. In expert-blinded benchmarking on 45 high-complexity cases across six foundation models, HemaGuide substantially improved concordance with tumor board decisions. A systematic ablation study across 11 layers confirmed that performance gains were routing-type-dependent, with no single component sufficient across case types. Automated classification of 70 clinically relevant missense variants showed high concordance with expert standards; no oncogenic variant was downgraded to benign and the whole workflow was completed under real-time conditions on commodity hardware with a median latency of 39 s rather than the hours typically required for manual molecular board workflows. In a simulated practice study, agent-assisted resident physicians achieved near-senior concordance and partially outperformed senior physicians in their subspecialty. External validation on 555 independent cases from a second academic center yielded 81.8% concordance across 47 entities, and a prospective 1-month silent trial on 64 consecutive, unselected cases achieved 82.8% concordance. Hallucinations occurred in 2 of 664 evaluated cases (0.3%). Together these data provide evidence that locally deployable, case-grounded large language model agents can deliver auditable clinical decision support across hematological malignancies, with concordance maintained across institutions and under real-time conditions on commodity hardware.
