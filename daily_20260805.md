### 1. DBT-DINO：迈向基于基础模型的数字乳腺断层合成分析

- **英文标题**: DBT-DINO: Toward Foundation Model-Based Analysis of Digital Breast Tomosynthesis.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Felix J Dorfner, Manon A Dorster, Ryan Connolly
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 9/10 — 医学影像AI基础模型，专用于数字乳腺断层合成（DBT），核心应用于乳腺癌筛查，符合高分方向。
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550018/

**中文摘要**: 背景：基础模型在医学影像领域展现出应用前景，但在三维模态中的探索仍显不足。尽管数字乳腺断层合成（DBT）已应用于乳腺癌筛查，但目前尚无针对该模态的专用基础模型。目的：开发并评估一种针对DBT的基础模型（DBT-DINO），并评估领域特异性预训练在多个临床任务中的影响。材料与方法：本回顾性研究使用了马萨诸塞州总布列根医院于2011年3月至2024年2月期间获取的DBT影像。采用Meta AI的DINOv2方法，对来自27 990例患者的487 975个DBT容积中的超过2 500万个二维切面进行自监督预训练。评估了三个下游任务：(a) 使用5 000次筛查检查进行乳腺密度分类；(b) 使用106 417次筛查检查预测5年内发生经活检证实的乳腺癌的风险；(c) 使用393个已标注容积进行病灶检测。采用McNemar检验和DeLong检验将DBT-DINO的性能与基于ImageNet预训练的DINOv2基线进行比较。结果：共纳入4 981例患者（平均年龄57.76岁±11.40 [标准差]；4 855例女性）进行密度分类，31 561例患者（平均年龄60.09岁±10.47；31 559例女性）进行风险预测，以及199例女性患者进行病灶检测。在乳腺密度分类中，DBT-DINO的准确率为79%（786/997次检查），优于DINOv2基线的73%（728/997次检查；P < .001）。在5年乳腺癌风险预测方面，DBT-DINO的受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUC）为0.78，DINOv2为0.76（P = .057），两者差异无统计学意义。在病灶检测中，DINOv2的平均敏感度为67%（91/136个病灶），而DBT-DINO的敏感度为62%（84/136个病灶）（P = .60），同样差异无统计学意义。结论：DBT-DINO在乳腺密度分类中表现出较强的性能；然而，在5年乳腺癌风险预测和病灶检测方面，与ImageNet基线相比差异无统计学意义，提示针对局部检测任务的领域特异性预训练仍需进一步优化。© RSNA, 2026 本文附有补充材料。另见本期Wu所撰写的述评。

**英文摘要**: Background Foundation models show promise in medical imaging but remain underexplored in three-dimensional modalities. Despite the adoption of digital breast tomosynthesis (DBT) in breast cancer screening, no dedicated foundation model currently exists for this modality. Purpose To develop and evaluate a foundation model for DBT (DBT-DINO) and assess the impact of domain-specific pretraining across multiple clinical tasks. Materials and Methods This retrospective study used DBT images from Mass General Brigham acquired between March 2011 and February 2024. Self-supervised pretraining was performed using Meta AI's DINOv2 methodology on more than 25 million two-dimensional sections from 487 975 DBT volumes from 27 990 patients. Three downstream tasks were evaluated: (a) breast density classification using 5000 screening examinations, (b) 5-year risk of developing biopsy-proven breast cancer using 106 417 screening examinations, and (c) lesion detection using 393 annotated volumes. The performance of DBT-DINO was compared with that of ImageNet-pretrained DINOv2 baselines using McNemar and DeLong tests. Results A total of 4981 patients (mean age, 57.76 years ± 11.40 [SD]; 4855 female) were included for density classification, 31 561 patients (mean age, 60.09 years ± 10.47; 31 559 female) were included for risk prediction, and 199 female patients were included for lesion detection. For breast density classification, DBT-DINO achieved 79% (786 of 997 examinations) accuracy, outperforming the DINOv2 baseline (73% [728 of 997 examinations]; P < .001). For 5-year breast cancer risk prediction, DBT-DINO had an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) of 0.78 and DINOv2 had an AUC of 0.76 ( P = .057), showing no evidence of a difference. In lesion detection, DINOv2 had an average sensitivity of 67% (91 of 136 lesions), whereas DBT-DINO had a sensitivity of 62% (84 of 136 lesions) ( P = .60), again with no evidence of a difference. Conclusion DBT-DINO demonstrated strong performance in breast density classification; however, there was no evidence of a difference compared with the ImageNet baseline in 5-year breast cancer risk prediction or lesion detection, suggesting that domain-specific pretraining for localized detection tasks required further refinement. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article. ### 2. 基于大型语言模型的放射学相关临床病史总结：一项多阅片者性能研究。

- **英文标题**: Radiologically Relevant Clinical History Summarization with Large Language Models: A Multireader Performance Study.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Adrian Serapio, Timothy L Chen, Brian Tangsombatvisit
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 9/10 — LLM结合医学影像临床历史摘要生成，直接提升放射科工作流与诊断价值，属核心临床AI应用。
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550025/

**中文摘要**: **背景**：随影像检查申请单一同提供的临床病史有助于指导检查方案的选择和诊断重点。然而，这些病史信息往往不完整，可能影响诊断准确性和工作流程效率。

**目的**：评估大语言模型（LLM）能否通过利用临床记录来改善所提供的影像检查适应证（indications）的临床实用性。

**材料与方法**：本回顾性研究从加州大学旧金山分校（2012年1月至2024年8月）的去标识化电子健康档案中整理了一个数据集，包含放射学报告，以及与之配对的、由转诊临床医生提供和由放射科医生整理的适应证，并关联至临床记录。该数据集按五个身体系统和五个病理生理学类别进行分层，以构建LLM选择和读片者研究内部测试集。在读片者研究中，20名具有2至25年经验的放射科医生对转诊临床医生、放射科医生和表现最佳的LLM所提供的适应证进行比较。读片者就全面性、事实准确性和简洁性进行评分，并对适应证在制定检查方案中的有用性、在影像解读中的有用性以及总体排名进行排序。采用累积链接混合模型（cumulative link mixed models）并进行Tukey校正的事后两两比较，对模型与临床医生的表现进行统计学比较。

**结果**：从28 313例患者（平均年龄59岁±20.6 [标准差]；14 912名女性）中，从247例患者中抽取了250项检查用于读片者研究。在排除9项后，共分析241项检查，得到482项读片者-检查配对评价。来自表现最佳的专有LLM（Claude 3.5 Sonnet；Anthropic）和开源LLM（Qwen 2.5-7B Instruct；阿里巴巴）的适应证在全面性（Likert评分为5的比例：分别为37.14%和28.42%；两者P均< .001）和事实准确性（分别为68.05%和59.75%；两者P均< .001）方面均高于转诊临床医生所提供的适应证。专有LLM在制定检查方案的有用性（排名第1的比例：40.87%；所有P均< .001）、影像解读的有用性（44.61%；所有P均< .001）和总体排名（44.19%；所有P均< .001）方面排名最高。全面性（占所有评分的65.77%；两者P均< .001）对总体排名的影响最为显著。

**结论**：LLM能够根据临床记录生成与放射学相关的适应证，其全面性和事实准确性均优于临床医生所提供的适应证；当由专有LLM生成时，其在制定检查方案和影像解读中的有用性排名最高。

© RSNA，2026
本文附有补充材料。另见本期Yilmaz和Cardoza-Ochoa所撰写的社论。

**英文摘要**: Background Clinical histories accompanying imaging orders guide protocol selection and diagnostic focus. However, they are often incomplete, potentially compromising diagnostic accuracy and workflow efficiency. Purpose To evaluate whether large language models (LLMs) can improve the clinical utility of provided imaging indications by leveraging clinical notes. Materials and Methods This retrospective study curated a dataset from deidentified electronic health records at the University of California San Francisco (January 2012 to August 2024), consisting of radiology reports with paired referring clinician-provided and radiologist-curated indications linked to clinical notes. The dataset was stratified across five body systems and five pathophysiologic categories to derive LLM selection and reader study internal test sets. For the reader study, 20 radiologists with 2-25 years of experience compared indications from the referring clinician, radiologist, and best-performing LLMs. Readers scored comprehensiveness, factuality, and conciseness and ranked indications for usefulness in protocoling, usefulness in interpretation, and overall ranking. Models and clinicians were compared using cumulative link mixed models with Tukey-adjusted post hoc comparisons. Results From 28 313 patients (mean age, 59 years ± 20.6 [SD]; 14 912 women), 250 examinations from 247 patients were sampled for the reader study. After nine exclusions, 241 examinations were analyzed, yielding 482 reader-examination evaluations. Indications from the best-performing proprietary (Claude 3.5 Sonnet; Anthropic) and open-source (Qwen 2.5-7B Instruct; Alibaba) LLM were rated as more comprehensive (Likert rating of 5: 37.14% and 28.42%, respectively; both P < .001) and factual (68.05% and 59.75%; both P < .001) than referring clinician indications. The proprietary LLM ranked most useful in protocoling (rank 1: 40.87%; all P < .001), useful in interpretation (44.61%; all P < .001), and overall ranking (44.19%, all P < .001). Comprehensiveness (65.77% of ratings; both P < .001) most strongly influenced overall rankings. Conclusion LLMs generated radiology-relevant indications from clinical notes that were more comprehensive and factual than clinician indications, and when generated by the proprietary LLM, were ranked most useful in protocoling and imaging interpretation. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article. See also the editorial by Yilmaz and Cardoza-Ochoa in this issue.

### 3. 我的方法：理解与利用生成式人工智能进行胸部放射学报告

- **英文标题**: How I Do It: Understanding and Leveraging Generative AI for Chest Radiology Reporting.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Eun Kyoung Hong, Seungho Lee, Mizuki Nishino
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550023/

**中文摘要**: 生成式人工智能（AI）为协助放射科医生在诊断解读和工作流程方面开辟了新的可能性，特别是在自动化报告生成方面。胸部X光摄影凭借其高临床工作量、诊断复杂性以及大型公共数据集的可用性，已成为生成式AI的领先应用场景。本文介绍了一种基于实践经验的方法，用于理解和实施胸部X光摄影报告的生成式AI。文章概述了生成模型的关键概念和底层架构，随后提出了一个将AI定位为起草助手的临床框架，强调了其实用性和局限性。通过代表性示例，文章展示了生成式AI如何加速工作流程、促进报告一致性并支持受训者教育，同时讨论了幻觉等已知陷阱。文章探讨了支持有效的人机协同审核的实施策略、界面设计考量及安全检查点。最后，文章展望了未来发展方向，强调情境感知建模、可解释性和多机构评估的重要性。旨在帮助放射科医生掌握基本知识和见解，以便在胸部放射学实践中安全有效地使用生成式AI工具，并以胸部X光摄影作为更广泛诊断影像整合的范式。

**英文摘要**: Generative artificial intelligence (AI) has introduced new possibilities for assisting radiologists in diagnostic interpretation and workflow, particularly through automated report generation. Chest radiography has emerged as a leading use case for generative AI due to its high clinical volume, its diagnostic complexity, and the availability of large public datasets. This article describes a practical, experience-based approach to understanding and implementing generative AI for chest radiograph reporting. It outlines key concepts and the underlying architecture of generative models, followed by a clinical framework that positions AI as a draft assistant, highlighting both its utility and limitations. Through representative examples, the article demonstrates how generative AI can accelerate workflow, promote reporting consistency, and support trainee education, while also addressing known pitfalls such as hallucinations. It discusses implementation strategies, interface considerations, and safety checkpoints that support effective human-in-the-loop review. The article concludes with future directions that emphasize the importance of context-aware modeling, explainability, and multi-institutional evaluation. The aim is to equip radiologists with essential knowledge and insights to engage safely and effectively with generative AI tools in thoracic radiology practice, using chest radiography as a paradigm for broader integration across diagnostic imaging.

### 4. 超快速深部三维组织学技术实现胶质瘤浸润的术中定位

- **英文标题**: Ultrarapid deep 3D histology enables intraoperative mapping of glioma infiltration.
- **期刊**: Cell (2026)
- **作者**: Zhijie Liu, Yingying Li, Lingchao Chen
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42546689/

**中文摘要**: 三维（3D）组织学可对整个标本中的组织微结构提供体积层面的深入洞察，在实现更精准预后判断方面具有巨大潜力。然而，现有方法由于速度过慢，无法满足术中会诊的需求。我们提出了ULTRA（超快透明化受激拉曼结合人工智能）平台，这是一种无标记、无染色、无固定、无切片的平台，利用受激拉曼散射（SRS）显微镜的化学特异性实现快速三维组织学分析。通过协同开发一步式组织透明化方案与无监督学习算法，ULTRA可在30分钟内提供高分辨率、达到福尔马林固定石蜡包埋（FFPE）切片质量级别的深层三维虚拟组织学成像，其覆盖的组织体积比基于切片的传统方法高出数个数量级。在人类手术切除的胶质瘤样本中，ULTRA能够精确解析三维组织学关键特征，并以单细胞分辨率描绘随深度变化的肿瘤浸润边界。通过将三维组织学的时间尺度从数天或数小时压缩至术中可及范围，ULTRA填补了关键的临床空白，能够在手术室内为更明智的外科决策提供支持。

**英文摘要**: Three-dimensional (3D) histology provides volumetric insights into tissue microarchitectures across entire specimens, holding great promise for more accurate prognostication. However, existing methods are too slow for intraoperative consultations. We present ULTRA (ultrarapid cleared stimulated Raman with AI), a label-free, stain-free, fixation-free, and section-free platform that leverages the chemical specificity of stimulated Raman scattering (SRS) microscopy for rapid 3D histological analysis. Through the synergistic development of a one-step tissue-clearing protocol and unsupervised learning algorithms, ULTRA delivers high-resolution, formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded (FFPE)-grade deep 3D virtual histology within 30 min, covering orders of magnitude more tissue than slide-based methods. In human surgical glioma samples, ULTRA accurately resolves key histological features in 3D and delineates depth-dependent tumor infiltration margins at single-cell resolution. By compressing 3D histology from days or hours to an intraoperative timescale, ULTRA addresses a critical clinical gap and enables more informed surgical decision-making in the operating room.

### 5. 基于睡眠的风险分层与临床结局基础模型。

- **英文标题**: A foundation model for sleep-based risk stratification and clinical outcomes.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Erhan Bilal, Matheus Lima Diniz Araujo, Kristen L Beck
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42547512/

**中文摘要**: 临床睡眠研究能够采集多种生理信号，然而其解读往往被简化为有限预后价值的单一汇总指标，如呼吸暂停低通气指数。我们提出了一种基础模型，该模型从超过10,000例与电子病历相关联的临床睡眠记录中学习睡眠生理学的丰富表征。在此我们证明，睡眠生理学包含传统指标无法揭示的潜在风险结构，能够识别出五个在死亡率、心血管疾病和神经系统疾病方面具有显著不同轨迹的患者风险组。最高风险组的死亡风险是最低风险组的两倍以上，而呼吸暂停低通气指数的严重程度分级则显示出有限的预测价值。该框架可推广至独立的睡眠心脏健康研究，尽管数据分辨率较低，仍能区分高风险与低风险患者。我们证明，基础模型能够从常规睡眠记录中提取传统指标系统性遗漏的、具有临床意义的风险信息，为精准睡眠医学提供了一条可扩展的路径。

**英文摘要**: Clinical sleep studies capture multiple physiologic signals, yet interpretation is often reduced to single summary measures of limited prognostic value, such as the apnea-hypopnea index. We present a foundation model that learns rich representations of sleep physiology from more than 10,000 clinical sleep recordings linked to electronic medical records. Here we show that sleep physiology contains latent risk structure invisible to conventional metrics, identifying five patient risk groups with markedly different trajectories for mortality, cardiovascular, and neurological disease. The highest-risk group shows more than double the mortality risk of the lowest, whereas apnea-hypopnea index severity categories show limited predictive value. The framework generalizes to the independent Sleep Heart Health Study, distinguishing high- and low-risk patients despite lower-resolution data. We demonstrate that foundation models recover clinically meaningful risk information embedded in routine sleep recordings that conventional metrics systematically miss, providing a scalable path to precision sleep medicine.
