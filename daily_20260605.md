### 1. 手语叙事揭示了皮层时间尺度层次的普遍性和模态特异性特征。

- **英文标题**: Sign language narrative reveals universal and modality-specific features of cortical timescale hierarchy.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Maria Zimmermann, Piotr Tomaszewski, Artur Marchewka
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42236497/

**中文摘要**: 人类语言在讲述故事方面具有独特性。先前的神经影像学研究表明，口语叙事理解涉及一个神经时间尺度层级，从单词、句子到叙事。手语与口语共享核心特征，但也具有模态特异性元素，例如使用手、面部和空间位置来传达意义并标记语法。在此，我们利用自然主义功能性磁共振成像（fMRI）来探究语言层级的哪些方面是言语特异性的，哪些是模态通用的。母语手语使用者（n=20）在fMRI扫描过程中观看了《厄舍府的倒塌》的20分钟波兰手语解说版本及其逐渐打乱后的版本。我们表明，手语叙事同步了层级神经网络，揭示了额颞区域（追踪句子级结构）与默认模式网络（追踪情节）之间的一种模态不变的分离。我们识别出两个此前在言语中未报告的特征：顶叶上回在较长的话语时间尺度上同步，而腹侧枕叶皮层则对单词和句子级处理做出反应。这些结果揭示了语言神经生物学中既存在普遍性，也可能存在模态特异性的方面。

**英文摘要**: Human languages are unique in their capacity to tell stories. Prior neuroimaging studies show that spoken language narrative comprehension engages a neural time-scale hierarchy, from words and sentences to narratives. Sign languages share core features with spoken languages but also have modality-specific elements such as use of hands, face and spatial position to convey meaning and mark grammar. Here, we use naturalistic fMRI to examine which aspects of the language hierarchy are speech-specific versus modality-general. Native signers (n = 20) watched a 20-minute Polish Sign Language interpretation of The Fall of the House of Usher and its progressively scrambled versions during fMRI. We show that signed narratives synchronize hierarchical neural networks, revealing a modality-invariant dissociation between frontotemporal regions, which track sentence-level structure, and the Default Mode Network tracking plot. We identify two signatures not previously reported for speech: the superior parietal cortex synchronizes at long discourse timescales, while the ventral occipital cortex responds to word- and sentence-level processing. These results reveal both universal and potentially modality-specific aspects of language neurobiology.

### 2. ReclAIm：一种用于监测和纠正医学影像AI性能下降的多智能体框架。

- **英文标题**: ReclAIm: A Multi-Agent Framework for Monitoring and Correcting Performance Decline in Medical Imaging AI.
- **期刊**: Radiology. Artificial intelligence (2026)
- **作者**: Eleftherios Tzanis, Michail E Klontzas
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42233761/

**中文摘要**: 目的 开发并评估一种用于医学图像分类模型性能衰退自动监测、检测与校正的多智能体框架（ReclAIm）。  
材料与方法 ReclAIm是一种基于大语言模型的多智能体系统，通过自然语言交互运行。一个主智能体协调三个任务特定智能体，执行性能评估，并在检测到显著性能衰退时触发微调。微调工作流程整合了数据增强、类别不平衡处理以及参数锚定正则化策略，以限制灾难性遗忘。该系统使用多个影像数据集进行基准测试，包括脑部MRI、胸部CT和胸部X线摄影，数据集按60%:20%:20%的比例划分为模型开发集、推理（性能监测）集和微调集。  
结果 ReclAIm成功统筹了所有数据集的训练、评估和性能监测。18个模型中有8个模型在测试数据与推理数据之间检测到性能差异，触发了微调工作流程，从而缩小了性能差距。在性能衰退高达40.6%的案例（心脏肥大数据集，InceptionV3）中，微调将性能指标恢复至基线值±2%以内。  
结论 ReclAIm为医学图像分类模型的自动监测与目标微调提供了原型框架，其自然语言接口设计旨在支持研究的可及性及潜在的临床应用。© RSNA, 2026。

**英文摘要**: Purpose To develop and evaluate a multiagent framework (ReclAIm) for automated monitoring, detection, and correction of performance decline in medical image classification models. Materials and Methods ReclAIm is a large language model-based multiagent system that operates through natural language interaction. A master agent coordinating three task-specific agents performed performance evaluation and triggered fine-tuning when substantial performance declines were detected. The fine-tuning workflow incorporated data augmentation, class imbalance handling, and a parameter-anchoring regularization strategy to limit catastrophic forgetting. The system was benchmarked using multiple imaging datasets, including brain MRI, chest CT, and chest radiography, partitioned into model development, inference (performance monitoring), and fine-tuning subsets (60%:20%:20%). Results ReclAIm successfully orchestrated training, evaluation, and performance monitoring across all datasets. Performance discrepancies between test and inference data were detected in 8 of 18 models, prompting fine-tuning workflows that reduced performance gaps. In cases with declines of up to 40.6% (cardiomegaly dataset, InceptionV3), fine-tuning restored performance metrics to within ± 2% of baseline values. Conclusion ReclAIm provides a prototype framework for automated monitoring and targeted fine-tuning of medical image classification models, with a natural language interface designed to support accessibility in research and potential clinical applications. © RSNA, 2026.

### 3. 非理想测量CT通用增强的影像基础模型

- **英文标题**: Imaging foundation model for universal enhancement of non-ideal measurement CT.
- **期刊**: Nature communications (2026)
- **作者**: Rongjun Ge, Yuxin Liu, Zhan Wu
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42236476/

**中文摘要**: 非理想测量计算机断层扫描采用次优成像协议以扩展CT的应用范围。然而，由此带来的权衡导致图像质量下降，限制了其临床可接受性。尽管深度学习方法已被用于增强非理想测量CT图像，但它们对大规模训练数据集的依赖以及在多样化场景中的泛化能力有限，阻碍了其实际应用。我们提出了多尺度集成Transformer放大模块（TAMP），一个用于通用非理想测量CT增强的成像基础模型。该模型在1080万张物理驱动的模拟非理想测量CT图像上进行了预训练，能够有效泛化到各种非理想测量CT设置、缺陷程度及身体部位。此外，一种参数高效的微调策略使得TAMP仅需少量切片即可适应特定临床场景。包括放射科医生评估和实际验证在内的大量实验表明，TAMP持续改善了图像质量和临床可接受性，凸显了其在推进CT成像及拓宽非理想测量CT在临床实践中应用的巨大潜力。

**英文摘要**: Non-ideal measurement computed tomography (CT) employs suboptimal imaging protocols to expand CT applications. However, the resulting trade-offs degrade image quality, limiting clinical acceptability. Although deep learning methods have been used to enhance non-ideal measurement CT images, their reliance on large training datasets and limited generalizability across diverse settings hinder practical use. We propose the multi-scale integrated Transformer Amplifier (TAMP), an imaging foundation model for universal non-ideal measurement CT enhancement. Pre-trained on 10.8 million physics-driven simulated non-ideal measurement CT images, TAMP generalizes effectively across various non-ideal measurement CT settings, defect degrees, and body regions. Moreover, a parameter-efficient fine-tuning strategy enables TAMP to adapt to specific clinical scenarios using only few slices. Extensive experiments, including radiologists and real-world validations, demonstrate that TAMP consistently improves image quality and clinical acceptability, underscoring its significant potential to advance CT imaging and broaden non-ideal measurement CT applications in clinical practice.

### 4. 基于人工智能的薄层重建技术对筛查型数字乳腺断层合成诊断准确性的影响。

- **英文标题**: Impact of AI-based Slab Reconstruction Technology on the Diagnostic Accuracy of Screening Digital Breast Tomosynthesis.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Manisha Bahl, Sarah Mercaldo, Leslie R Lamb
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42227862/

**中文摘要**: 背景：数字乳腺断层合成（DBT）采用1毫米切片，导致相较于传统二维数字乳腺摄影，图像数量显著增加，判读时间延长。切片重建技术通过生成更厚的切片，减少了需要解读的图像数量，提高了效率并降低了存储需求，从而应对这一挑战。

目的：比较基于人工智能（AI）的切片重建技术实施前后，DBT筛查诊断准确性的差异。

材料与方法：回顾性分析某学术医学中心在切片重建技术实施前后，连续进行的筛查性DBT检查数据。该切片重建技术利用AI生成6毫米的合成切片，重叠层厚3毫米，像素分辨率为70微米。技术实施前阶段为2018年1月至2019年12月，实施后阶段为2021年10月至2022年9月。采用多变量Logistic回归模型比较两个阶段的筛查性能指标，并进行非劣效性分析。

结果：共分析了64,949名女性的119,662次筛查性DBT检查，其中实施前阶段77,577次（52,649名女性；平均年龄60岁±11 [标准差]），实施后阶段42,085次（42,059名女性；平均年龄60岁±11）。两个阶段的癌症检出率（CDR）（每1000次检查中5.8 vs 6.5；校正比值比[OR]，1.1；P = 0.49）、灵敏度（82.3% vs 85.9%；校正OR，1.3；P = 0.27）和假阴性率（每1000次检查中1.2 vs 1.1；校正OR，0.8；P = 0.39）均无显著差异，且三项指标均满足非劣效性标准。实施后阶段异常判读率（AIR）较低（6.2% vs 5.8%；校正OR，0.9；P < 0.001），特异性较高（94.4% vs 94.9%；校正OR，1.1；P < 0.001）。

结论：基于AI的切片重建技术的实施与不劣于传统方法的CDR和灵敏度、更高的特异性以及更低的AIR相关。©作者2026年。由北美放射学会根据CC BY 4.0许可协议发布。本文附有补充材料。另见本期Grimm撰写的社论。

**英文摘要**: Background Digital breast tomosynthesis (DBT) uses 1-mm slices, resulting in a larger number of images and longer interpretation times than conventional digital two-dimensional mammography. Slab reconstruction technologies address this challenge by generating thicker slices, thereby reducing the number of images requiring review, improving efficiency, and lowering storage demand. Purpose To compare the diagnostic accuracy of screening DBT before and after the implementation of artificial intelligence (AI)-based slab reconstruction technology. Materials and Methods Consecutive screening DBT examinations obtained before and after the implementation of a slab reconstruction technology at an academic medical center were retrospectively reviewed. The slab reconstruction technology uses AI to generate 6-mm synthetic slices with 3-mm overlap and 70-μm pixel resolution. The preimplementation period was between January 2018 and December 2019, and the postimplementation period was between October 2021 and September 2022. Multivariable logistic regression models were used to compare screening performance metrics in both periods, and a noninferiority analysis was performed. Results A total of 119 662 screening DBT examinations in 64 949 women were analyzed: 77 577 (52 649 women; mean age, 60 years ± 11 [SD]) during the preimplementation period and 42 085 (42 059 women; mean age, 60 years ± 11) during the postimplementation period. The cancer detection rate (CDR) (5.8 vs 6.5 per 1000 examinations; adjusted odds ratio [OR], 1.1; P = .49), sensitivity (82.3% vs 85.9%; adjusted OR, 1.3; P = .27), and false-negative rate (1.2 vs 1.1 per 1000 examinations; adjusted OR, 0.8; P = .39) did not differ between periods, and all three metrics met the noninferiority criteria. The abnormal interpretation rate (AIR) was lower (6.2% vs 5.8%; adjusted OR, 0.9; P < .001) and the specificity was higher (94.4% vs 94.9%; adjusted OR, 1.1; P < .001) during the postimplementation period. Conclusion The implementation of AI-based slab reconstruction technology was associated with noninferior CDR and sensitivity, improved specificity, and reduced AIR. © The Author(s) 2026. Published by the Radiological Society of North America under a CC BY 4.0 license. Supplemental material is available for this article. See also the editorial by Grimm in this issue.

### 5. 基于人工智能的心脏磁共振成像微血管阻塞组织学异质性预测主要不良心血管事件：一项多中心研究。

- **英文标题**: AI-based Histologic Heterogeneity of Microvascular Obstruction at Cardiac MRI for Predicting MACEs: A Multicenter Study.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Bing-Hua Chen, Shu-Lin Li, Jin-Yi Xiang
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42227866/

**中文摘要**: 背景 微血管阻塞（MVO）与ST段抬高型心肌梗死（STEMI）后的不良预后密切相关。然而，人工量化MVO不仅耗时，且无法捕捉微血管损伤的异质性。目的 评估一种基于人工智能（AI）的模型，该模型包括自动化MVO分割和影像组学特征提取，以解码微血管损伤异质性，并评估其预测主要不良心血管事件（MACEs）的能力。材料与方法 这项多中心回顾性研究（2013年6月至2023年12月）纳入了接受心脏MRI检查的STEMI合并MVO患者。应用先前开发的AI模型进行自动化MVO分析，随后通过最小绝对收缩和选择算子（LASSO）回归进行降维，构建影像组学评分（radscore）。主要结局为MACEs，包括心血管死亡、心肌再梗死、恶性心律失常和因心力衰竭住院。进行限制性立方样条分析以检验radscore与MACE风险之间潜在的非线性关系。结果 在843例STEMI患者中（中位年龄60岁[IQR，51-67岁]；760名男性；训练集n=387；验证集n=166；外部测试集n=290），190例发生MACEs。AI模型对MRI扫描进行了分割，从中提取了1595个影像组学特征，并通过LASSO回归获得6个特征用于构建radscore。发生MACEs的患者radscore高于未发生者（均值，-0.98±0.50 [SD] vs -1.42±0.50；P < .001）。与传统MVO体积量化相比，radscore显示出更高的预后价值。Radscore成为MACEs的独立预测因子（风险比，4.20 [95% CI：3.19，5.53]；P < .001），并有助于优化风险分层。将radscore与传统变量整合可提升预后性能，C指数从仅使用传统变量的0.77（95% CI：0.73，0.81）增加到传统变量联合radscore的0.80（95% CI：0.77，0.83）（P < .001）。结论 AI自动化的MVO影像组学分析能有效预测MACE风险，且优于传统定量评估。© RSNA, 2026 本文可获取补充材料。

**英文摘要**: Background Microvascular obstruction (MVO) is strongly associated with adverse outcomes after ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI). However, manual quantification of MVO is time-consuming and fails to capture the heterogeneity of microvascular injury. Purpose To evaluate an artificial intelligence (AI)-based model including automated MVO segmentation and radiomic feature extraction to decode microvascular damage heterogeneity, and to assess its ability to predict major adverse cardiovascular events (MACEs). Materials and Methods This multicenter retrospective study (June 2013-December 2023) included patients with STEMI and MVO who underwent cardiac MRI. A previously developed AI model was applied for automated MVO analysis, followed by least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) regression for dimensionality reduction to construct a radiomic score (radscore). The primary outcome was MACEs, including cardiovascular death, myocardial reinfarction, malignant arrhythmia, and hospitalization for heart failure. Restricted cubic spline analysis was performed to examine the potentially nonlinear relationship between the radscore and MACE risk. Results Among the 843 patients with STEMI (median age, 60 years [IQR, 51-67 years]; 760 male patients; training set, n = 387; validation set, n = 166; external test set, n = 290), 190 experienced MACEs. The AI model segmented the MRI scans, from which 1595 radiomic features were extracted, and LASSO regression was used to obtain six features for constructing the radscore. Patients with MACEs had higher radscores than those without MACEs (mean, -0.98 ± 0.50 [SD] vs -1.42 ± 0.50; P < .001). Compared with conventional MVO volume quantification, the radscore demonstrated greater prognostic value. The radscore emerged as an independent predictor of MACEs (hazard ratio, 4.20 [95% CI: 3.19, 5.53]; P < .001) and contributed to optimizing risk stratification. Integrating the radscore with conventional variables enhanced prognostic performance, with the C index increasing from 0.77 (95% CI: 0.73, 0.81) for conventional variables alone to 0.80 (95% CI: 0.77, 0.83) for conventional variables plus radscore ( P < .001). Conclusion AI-automated MVO radiomic analysis effectively predicted MACE risk and outperformed conventional quantitative assessment. © RSNA, 2026 Supplemental material is available for this article.
