### 1. MR-AIV 通过物理信息人工智能揭示活体全脑液体流动。

- **英文标题**: MR-AIV reveals in vivo brain-wide fluid flow with physics-informed AI.
- **期刊**: Science advances (2026)
- **作者**: Juan Diego Toscano, Yisen Guo, Zhibo Wang
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42202031/

**中文摘要**: 脑脊液和组织间隙液的循环在清除脑内代谢废物中起着关键作用，其功能紊乱与神经系统疾病密切相关。然而，直接测量全脑范围的液体运输（尤其是深部脑区）一直难以实现。本文提出磁共振人工智能测速（MR-AIV）框架，该框架采用基于物理信息的专用架构与优化方法，可从动态对比增强磁共振成像（DCE-MRI）中重建三维流体速度场。MR-AIV不仅能揭示全脑速度图谱，还能提供组织渗透率和压力场等传统方法无法获取的参数。将其应用于脑部研究后，MR-AIV展示了组织间隙和血管周围流动的功能性景观，定量区分了缓慢的扩散驱动运输（约0.1微米/秒）与快速平流流动（约3微米/秒）。该方法为探究健康与疾病状态下的脑部清除机制及流体动力学开辟了新途径，并有望广泛应用于从地球物理学到组织力学等其他多孔介质系统。

**英文摘要**: The circulation of cerebrospinal and interstitial fluid plays a vital role in clearing metabolic waste from the brain, and its disruption has been linked to neurological disorders. However, directly measuring brain-wide fluid transport, especially in the deep brain, has remained elusive. Here, we introduce magnetic resonance artificial intelligence velocimetry (MR-AIV), a framework featuring a specialized physics-informed architecture and optimization method that reconstructs three-dimensional fluid velocity fields from dynamic contrast-enhanced magnetic resonance imaging (DCE-MRI). MR-AIV unveils brain-wide velocity maps while providing estimates of tissue permeability and pressure fields, quantities inaccessible to other methods. Applied to the brain, MR-AIV reveals a functional landscape of interstitial and perivascular flow, quantitatively distinguishing slow diffusion-driven transport [∼0.1 micrometers per second (μm/s)] from rapid advective flow (∼3 μm/s). This approach enables new investigations into brain clearance mechanisms and fluid dynamics in health and disease, with broad potential applications to other porous medium systems, from geophysics to tissue mechanics.

### 2. 放射学中可信赖人工智能的政策、实践与患者安全对齐

- **英文标题**: Alignment of Policy, Practice, and Patient Safety for Trustworthy AI in Radiology.
- **期刊**: Radiology. Artificial intelligence (2026)
- **作者**: Florence X Doo, Melissa Davis, Jason Poff
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42200797/

**中文摘要**: 人工智能（AI）已从技术研究阶段发展到常规临床使用，达到了一个技术能力可能超出当前监管和监督框架的转折点。这些系统正变得越来越复杂，从狭窄的、特定任务的算法演进为基础模型和早期的代理原型。这种演进重新分配了风险、责任和临床判断，要求放射科医生和医疗保健领导者理解政策选择如何影响患者安全和临床创新的推进。本特别报告提供了一个路线图，通过一个以患者安全为中心的、基于生命周期的实用框架，将政策与临床实践相结合。“转化双对齐”是一个概念，它将监管科学要求（AI系统向临床医生和患者提供的内容）与实施科学能力（机构为AI安全部署提供的条件）配对。该框架涵盖了AI的完整生命周期，从数据管理和模型开发到验证、部署和监控，并基于可信AI原则阐明了供应商、机构和临床医生的共同责任。分析侧重于美国监管框架，特别是美国食品药品监督管理局管理医疗AI的政策，并突出国际相关经验。文中提供了放射科医生参与政策制定、参与监督以及与行业和政策制定者合作的具体机会，以帮助塑造一个可信赖且可持续的AI生态系统。政策、实践和患者安全的对齐将使医疗AI对临床护理和公众信任产生持久影响。提供的分析和建议代表作者的观点，并不一定反映北美放射学会的官方立场。

**英文摘要**: Artificial intelligence (AI) has progressed from technical research to routine clinical use, reaching an inflection point where technological capabilities may exceed current regulatory and oversight frameworks. These systems are becoming more complex, progressing from narrow, task-specific algorithms to foundation models and early agentic prototypes. This progression has redistributed risk, responsibility, and clinical judgement, requiring radiologists and health care leaders to understand how policy choices affect patient safety and clinical innovation advancement. This Special Report provides a roadmap for aligning policy with clinical practice through a practical, lifecycle-based framework centered on patient safety. "Translational bialignment" is a concept which pairs regulatory science requirements (what AI systems deliver to clinicians and patients) with implementation science capabilities (what institutions provide to AI for safe deployment). This framework addresses the full AI lifecycle, from data stewardship and model development to validation, deployment, and monitoring, and articulates shared responsibilities for vendors, institutions, and clinicians grounded in trustworthy AI principles. The analysis focuses on U.S. regulatory frameworks, particularly Food and Drug Administration policies governing medical AI, with relevant highlights from international approaches. Concrete opportunities for radiologists to engage in policy formation, participate in oversight, and collaborate with industry and policymakers are provided to help shape a trustworthy and sustainable AI ecosystem. The alignment of policy, practice, and patient safety will enable medical AI to have a lasting impact on clinical care and public trust. The analysis and recommendations provided represent the authors' perspectives and do not necessarily reflect the official positions of the Radiological Society of North America.

### 3. 基于定向进化轨迹的深度学习导航高阶蛋白质适应度景观

- **英文标题**: Navigating high-order protein fitness landscapes via deep learning on directed evolution trajectories.
- **期刊**: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America (2026)
- **作者**: Chengzhi Song, Liang Ma, Lingfeng Xue
- **类别**: 临床决策/预测 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42190010/

**中文摘要**: 准确预测高阶突变的适应度效应是理解和设计蛋白质过程中的重大挑战。现有模型（包括预训练的蛋白质语言模型）难以捕捉决定这些效应的多残基相互作用。为此，我们提出DENet——一种深度学习框架，通过利用定向进化轨迹中丰富的共突变信息，重构高分辨率适应度景观，用于解析和设计复杂蛋白质变体。在肿瘤靶点KRAS的应用中，DENet引导的筛选系统性地识别出具有强活性的高阶突变体，并揭示了隐藏的别构机制。对于MEK1，DENet筛选出耐药性提升超1000倍的复杂变体，揭示了协同作用的尾部突变，并回溯性识别出超过75%的已知临床突变，性能显著优于现有模型。为扩展该框架的适用性，我们开发了一种计算模拟策略，通过模拟定向进化从广泛可用的单突变数据集中推断共突变信息。DENet提供了一个定量框架，用于导航复杂适应度景观，将多突变蛋白的理性设计与对其机制及临床意义的阐明相结合。

**英文摘要**: Accurately predicting the fitness effects of high-order mutations is a grand challenge in understanding and engineering proteins. Existing models, including pretrained protein language models, struggle to capture the multiresidue interactions that govern these effects. Here, we introduce DENet, a deep learning framework that harnesses the rich comutation information within directed evolution (DE) trajectories to reconstruct high-resolution fitness landscapes for deciphering and engineering of complex protein variants. Applied to the cancer target KRAS, DENet-guided screening systematically identified high-order mutants with potent activities and uncovered hidden allosteric mechanisms. For MEK1, DENet nominated complex variants with >1,000-fold increased drug resistance, revealed synergistic tail mutations, and retrospectively identified over 75% of known clinical mutations, largely outperforming existing models. To broaden the framework's applicability, we developed an in silico strategy that simulates directed evolution to infer comutation information from widely available single-mutant datasets. DENet provides a quantitative framework for navigating complex fitness landscapes, uniting the rational engineering of multimutation proteins with the elucidation of their mechanisms and clinical implications.

### 4. 基于CT基础模型预测肺叶气肿进展。

- **英文标题**: Prediction of Lobar Emphysema Progression with a CT-Based Foundational Model.
- **期刊**: Radiology (2026)
- **作者**: Ariel Hern&#xe1;n Curiale, Marc Niethammer, Ra&#xfa;l San Jos&#xe9; Est&#xe9;par
- **类别**: 医学影像 AI
- **相关性**: 6/10 — 评分失败，默认保留
- **原文链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42188737/

**中文摘要**: **背景** 肺气肿的进展具有空间异质性；慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）患者可能表现出相似的总体肺密度下降，但具有不同的区域疾病进展模式。然而，目前尚无可用工具来预测肺叶特异性密度变化，以量化和预测肺气肿随时间推移的进展。

**目的** 开发并评估一种基于胸部CT扫描的深度学习预后模型，以帮助预测COPD参与者的肺叶肺密度下降，并评估肺叶肺气肿进展。

**材料与方法** 在这项前瞻性研究中，纳入来自慢性阻塞性肺疾病遗传流行病学（COPDGene）研究和纵向评估COPD以确定预测性替代终点（ECLIPSE）研究的5823名有或无COPD的参与者，这些参与者接受了系列胸部CT检查。在来自COPDGene训练集的2136名参与者的子集CT扫描上训练了一个基础模型，以识别指示肺气肿进展的局部影像学特征。该基于肺叶的模型设计采用全局注意力机制来捕捉肺叶组织破坏，并在来自COPDGene研究的2718名参与者的内部测试集和来自ECLIPSE研究的969名参与者的外部测试集中进行了评估。CT扫描被统一为20 HU噪声分布，与COPDGene高剂量方案一致。预测的年化体积调整肺密度变化（ΔALD，g/L/年）值与观察值进行比较，采用均方根误差和皮尔逊相关性（r），并使用Bland-Altman图评估一致性。

**结果** 本研究共纳入5823名参与者（平均年龄60岁±8.6 [标准差]；3003名男性）。在COPDGene内部测试集（各肺叶平均皮尔逊相关系数，r = 0.433 ± 0.054）和ECLIPSE外部测试集（各肺叶平均皮尔逊r = 0.471 ± 0.036）中，ΔALD的模型预测与观察到的肺叶下降呈正相关。在COPDGene内部测试集（各肺叶受试者工作特征曲线下平均面积 [AUC]，0.70 ± 0.013）和ECLIPSE外部测试集（平均AUC，0.70 ± 0.009）中，基于预测ΔALD值，该模型在识别肺气肿加速进展的肺叶方面表现相似。

**结论** 开发了一种基于CT的肺叶密度下降和肺气肿进展的预后模型，并在接受胸部CT检查的COPD参与者中进行了评估，模型预测与观察值呈正相关。

临床试验注册号：NCT00608764 和 NCT0029255210 © 作者 2026。由北美放射学会根据CC BY 4.0许可发布。本文附有补充材料。另见本期Marrocchio和Lynch的社论。

**英文摘要**: Background Emphysema progression is spatially heterogeneous; individuals with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) may show similar overall declines in lung density but different regional patterns of disease progression. However, there are currently no tools available to predict lobe-specific density changes to quantify and forecast emphysema progression over time. Purpose To develop and evaluate a deep learning prognostic model using chest CT scans to help predict lobar lung density decline and assess lobar emphysema progression in participants with COPD. Materials and Methods In this prospective study, 5823 participants with and without COPD who underwent serial chest CT were included from the Genetic Epidemiology of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPDGene) and Evaluation of COPD Longitudinally to Identify Predictive Surrogate End-points (ECLIPSE) studies. A foundational model was trained on CT scans from a subset of 2136 participants from the COPDGene training set to identify local radiographic features indicative of emphysema progression. The lobe-based model was designed to leverage a global attention mechanism to capture lobar tissue destruction and was evaluated in an internal test set of 2718 participants from the COPDGene study and an external test set of 969 participants from the ECLIPSE study. CT scans were harmonized to a 20 HU noise profile, consistent with the COPDGene high-dose protocol. Predicted annualized change in volume-adjusted lung density (ΔALD, g/L per year) values were compared with observed values using root mean squared error and Pearson correlation ( r ), with agreement assessed using Bland-Altman plots. Results This study included 5823 participants (mean age, 60 years ± 8.6 [SD]; 3003 men). Model predictions of ΔALD showed a positive correlation with observed lobar decline in the COPDGene internal test set (mean Pearson correlation coefficient across lobes, r = 0.433 ± 0.054) and in the ECLIPSE external test set (mean Person r across lobes = 0.471 ± 0.036). The model performed similarly in identification of lobes with accelerated emphysema progression based on predicted ΔALD values in both the COPDGene internal test set (mean lobar area under the receiver operating characteristic curve [AUC], 0.70 ± 0.013) and the ECLIPSE external test set (mean AUC, 0.70 ± 0.009) across ΔALD risk values. Conclusion A CT-based prognostic model of lobe density decline and emphysema progression was developed and evaluated in participants with COPD at chest CT, and model predictions showed positive correlations with observed values.
